# Catheter staging rests

Original procedural full-size polymer geometry, authored for an inert bench trainer.
No third-party mesh, texture, logo or commercial product model is used.
`build_asset.py` constructs every collider and visual; `dimensions.json` records sizes.

These are open instrument rests for reproducible initial placement. They neither
clamp the instruments nor guide the receiver connection or catheter withdrawal.
The movable valve and catheter remain native free bodies joined by the existing
passive branch. Use does not establish a clinical procedure or hardware validation.

Version2 preserves the ordinary open-rest role. Tall posts stand at x=0.300/0.450m,
outside the loose valve sweep. Horizontal6mm-wide arms carry8mm-tall ledges beneath
the rear tab corners (y=-0.142m), with the original support top z=0.8535m.
No body joint, clamp, withdrawal channel or motion-dependent geometry is added.
The original package is preserved separately. This is original procedural geometry.
