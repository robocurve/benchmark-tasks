Each full-size receiving rest consists of two convex HDPE halves forming an open
90° V, with combined dimensions 20 × 28 × 27.3 mm. Groove bottom is 17.3 mm above
the foot; a 6 mm training shaft rests with its axis 21.543 mm above the foot.
The pair is 150 mm apart. There is no clamp, joint, drive, or attachment. Convex
colliders preserve the actual open groove rather than spanning it with a hull.
These are authored open receiving rests for the rigid mechanical trainer, not a
claim about flexible clinical catheter handling.
