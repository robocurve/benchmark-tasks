# Operation and comparison boundary

The physical goal is to empty a retention balloon into a free receiver before withdrawing
the retained catheter through a narrow neck. The valve and receiver must be held and
aligned, the measured piston return changes clearance, and the receiver must be physically
pulled off before final placement. Connected reverse travel can reinflate the balloon.

Comparison evidence consists of frozen board definitions, PR heads, source images and
primary public benchmark definitions stored with each independent review. PR creation
dates and local draft dates establish no official ownership. Exact catalog counts, source
revisions and unresolved coverage boundaries belong to those run artifacts, not this
static task description. Public-source comparison does not reproduce the official private
retrieval corpus or establish global novelty.

Relevant neighboring operations include connector insertion/removal, syringe preparation,
guide/probe movement, medical instrument transfer and fluid-device reset. Their actual
objects, manipulation and goal must be compared against the balloon/clearance operation.
No favorable review or official acceptance is asserted here.
