Replace the worn blade in the quick-release holder with the new blue blade and verify it is retained. 1. The worn blade (grey, red mark) is installed in the holder. The new blade (blue, white mark) lies in the rack beside it. 2. Turn the orange collar lever toward the arm bases by about 35 degrees, close to its 40-degree stop, and keep it turned. This lifts the red locking pin. 3. While the collar is held, pull the worn blade straight out of the holder and put it in the green tray. 4. Insert the blue blade, black edge down and the notched tang end first, straight into the holder until its shoulder stops against the holder nose. 5. Let the collar lever return to its rest stop. The pin drops into the blade's notch. 6. Retention check: hold the blue blade by its end grip block. Open the other gripper and move it at least 6 cm clear of the holder, collar and pin. Pull the blue blade straight outward. The holder slides on its spring carriage by 6 mm or more and must stay there for at least half a second while the blade stays seated. Then relax the pull. 7. Open both grippers and move them away from the holder and the blades.
