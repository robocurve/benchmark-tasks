# Reproducing an attended raised-cart handoff

Use a complete Clarke HTL500 mobile hydraulic lifting table, catalogue number **7610152**, with its original hydraulic circuit, release handle and two factory safety support bars. The manufacturer identifies the bars as item **51** and the release handle as item **65**. The HTL500 table has a 500 kg working-load rating. That figure applies to the assembled lift. Clarke specifies that the two bars carry the load during raised work. The manual gives no separate numeric capacity for an individual bar. [Clarke HTL300/HTL500 instructions, technical specifications, operation pp. 6–7, parts p. 12](https://cdn.clarkeinternational.com/documents/HTL500%20Lifting%20Table_Rev%205_%28020110152%29_%287610152%29.pdf)

## Equipment and layout

| Item | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| Cart | Intact factory unit on a level, hard floor. Inspect the ram, brakes, clips, pivots, both bars and their bearing surfaces. A damaged or incomplete unit is grounds to cancel the trial. Apply both caster brakes. |
| Load | One closed rigid crate, nominally 370 × 360 × 210 mm. Ballast and secure it internally so that the total is 20.0 kg, checked on a scale. Strap it against movement on the centered lateral line. In the model the crate center is 60 mm toward the handle end from the deck center. |
| Technician surrogate | A full-size workwear mannequin with a gloved hand, stable on an independently guided, outward-moving base. Use a cord or an externally controlled actuator to withdraw it from outside the cart perimeter. It carries no load. No human enters the scissor or deck sweep. |
| Instrumentation | Tape measure, scale, removable witness tape, two fixed cameras or phones on tripods, and a ruler in each measurement plane. Time-synchronize the views by filming one visible cue. One view must show both the fingertips and the deck edge. A side view must show the lever and the bar ends. Keep cameras, rulers and people outside the sweep. |
| Manipulation | The simulation uses two YAM arms; the hardware operator works from the normal external operating positions. For robotic reproduction, check external reach to both genuine bars and the handle at the chosen raised height before loading. Never fabricate a structural support or modify the cart to match the simulated grasp tabs. |

## Reset and trial

1. A qualified operator checks the unit against the manual for its model and revision. Start fully lowered with the mannequin outside the footprint. Position and secure the crate, engage the brakes, and clear the loading area. Record the unit identification, ballast mass and brake state.
2. With everyone clear, raise the table to the factory bar deployment height. Record this specimen's top height and clip/seat locations against a fixed ruler. Install removable visual witness marks from outside the mechanism. Use the factory geometry to choose the height. The simulated 825 mm deck-center coordinate is a model datum.
3. Start with both bars in their factory stow positions and pressure held. From outside the barrier, bring the dummy hand into view beneath the stationary deck. Record a side view showing the clearance above it and a plan view showing the overlap. The simulated gloved fingertips project approximately 84 mm inside the deck edge. A delayed or failed withdrawal is performed in simulation only.
4. During a physical demonstration, preserve pressure during withdrawal and deployment. The two operations can occur in either order while the deck stays still. Verify the entire dummy hand and sleeve are outside the moving envelope. The benchmark clearance mark is 20 mm beyond its outer edge. Verify both bars are positioned in their factory load paths, one on each leg. If either observation is ambiguous, keep the valve closed and suspend the trial.
5. From the normal handle position, operate the factory release slowly. Film the deck settling onto both factory bars. Confirm the bar ends bear as shown in the manual and clear the bolt heads marked B. Release the handle. Record at least two seconds of stable support with the manipulator clear of the load path. The endpoint is attended raised work.
6. For reset, keep the mannequin and all personnel outside the moving envelope. Raise only enough to unload the bars, return them to the factory clips from the safe external positions, then lower the platform fully under control. Remove the secured load at the lowered position. Follow the manufacturer’s procedure if a bar binds or leaks are visible.

## What a reviewer can check

Use the first frame showing effective lever actuation and the preceding frame to check withdrawal and the position of both bars. A shot of the final pose alone cannot establish the order. Record a pressure-held intermediate state with both bars positioned and an untouched lever for the 0.5 example. For the full example, use both views to see load transfer, the crate remaining within 15 mm of its witness marks, and no more than 3 mm of deck motion over the final two seconds. Fix the camera and film the ruler at deck height to control parallax. Inconclusive or occluded footage requires another observation. Dangerous counterexamples are simulation-only.

The simulation represents bar pivots on separate scissor legs, contact with the underside of the platform, and a spring-return lever. The documented cart supplies the load-bearing hardware for a physical trial. The rendered tabs, lever location, simplified ram contact, individual link masses and numerical force thresholds are simulator approximations. The virtual ram-pad clearance proves a separate load path in the model. On the production cart the cylinder stays connected; actual bar bearing during controlled release provides the field observation. A physical robot trial and dimensional survey of an HTL500 specimen have not been performed.
