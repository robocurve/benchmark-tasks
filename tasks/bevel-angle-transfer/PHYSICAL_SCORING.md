# Physical judging guide

Record one continuous video with the reference fixture, carry and marking visible. Keep the stock and blade edges visible at capture and board placement so their relative angles can be measured from the video. Take one overhead photo of the board after the trial with all four board edges visible. Use a millimetre ruler or the [actual-size overlay](scoring-overlay.svg) for the line. A ruler, a 3 mm feeler and a protractor can be used to check the transfer.

## Transfer

1. At the reference fixture, the stock face and the blade edge must fit their respective reference faces. Check the gap at both ends of each mating span. All four gaps must be at most 3 mm. Note the angle between the stock and blade.
2. While the gauge is still at the fixture, the blade gripper must visibly open and clear the blade and tab before the gauge leaves. The robot must lift and carry the gauge by the stock, with the gauge clear of intervening surfaces. Dragging, setting down during the carry, regrasping or readjusting the blade invalidates the transfer.
3. At board placement, the stock face must sit within 3 mm of the near edge across its overlap with the board, with the blade flat on the board (at most 3 mm of visible gap). Compare the stock-to-blade angle at placement with the angle at capture, using the same end of the stock and the same blade edge in both views. The difference must be at most 2°.

A failed transfer scores 0. A valid transfer earns 0.25 even if the marking does not qualify for a higher score.

## Line

Use the video to confirm the blade guided the marker with the stock seated against the board. Check the finished ink on the overhead photo or directly on the board. Exclude the preprinted red tick from the ink measurements.

| Check | 0.5 | 1.0 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Distance from the red tick centre to the nearest ink | ≤5 mm | ≤3 mm |
| Distinct marked coverage along the 140 mm board width | ≥70 mm | ≥126 mm |
| Perpendicular distance from the target centreline to every ink edge | ≤5 mm | ≤3 mm |
| Largest internal gap along the width axis | ≤5 mm | ≤3 mm |
| Maximum line width perpendicular to the target | ≤4 mm | ≤4 mm |
| Separate specks or a second line | None | None |
| Final gap from the nib tip to the board surface | — | ≥5 mm |

Project the ink onto the board width axis, from the stock edge toward the far edge. Count each marked interval once and add their widths. A blank interval between two marked intervals is an internal gap.

Measure line width between the outermost ink edges at each cross-section perpendicular to the target. To count as one interrupted line, adjacent fragments must follow each other along the target without overlapping along it, and their facing ends must fit within a common 4 mm width. The internal gap limit still applies. Award the highest score whose checks all pass.

## Overlay

Print the SVG at actual size and verify both 100 mm scales with a ruler. The overlay measures 140 × 180 mm. Align the edge labelled **Near edge** with the stock edge and the outlined tick with the red tick. Use transparent material, or align the graphic to the four corners of an overhead photo corrected for perspective. The solid line is the target. The dashed lines show the perpendicular tolerance bands. Use the horizontal millimetre scale to project the marked intervals and gaps. Apply the overlay after the trial. The board at reset shows the red tick only.
