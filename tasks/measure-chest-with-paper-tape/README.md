# Measure a torso's chest with a paper tape

The native demo scores **1.0**; Dream-1 scores **0.00**. Both full recorded episodes were rescored from their frozen sources.

Wrap a graduated paper tape around a torso form and present the zero-over-scale reading at the front. The tape starts loose on the table. There are no slots, guides, magnets or tape-to-torso attachments.

The physical output is a readable measurement, rather than a spoken or guessed number. The form is held by its ordinary dress-form base. Its chest cross-section is adult-sized: 240 mm front-to-back and 320 mm side-to-side. The upper-waist-to-neck form is 340 mm high. This measures taking a clothing measurement on a stationary form; it does not model a person breathing or moving.

## Setup

Use a rigid smooth plastic torso form generated by `geometry.py`, a 210 mm diameter by 16 mm base, and one 1080 × 20 × 0.20 mm strip of 160 gsm paper, printed on both faces with centimetre ticks and numbers every 5 cm. Cut from a continuous roll; do not splice the measuring portion. The exact form geometry is supplied by `export_setup.py`. Check print scale against a ruler before cutting.

The table is 747.5 mm high. The torso's centre is at x=180 mm, y=0, with the chest plane 145 mm above the table. Coordinates and every tape segment's starting pose are in `geometry.py`. Stand the tape on its lower long edge in the shallow curve shown in the setup image, behind the form from the measuring side. The curved footprint supports the 20 mm high strip; nothing holds it in place. Two small chest landmarks identify the measurement level; there is no circumferential guide.

The two YAM arms have 40 × 10 × 1 mm steel replacement precision fingers with thin silicone contact faces, mounted on a 100 × 10 × 2 mm steel leaf. The stated 1 mm thickness includes the facing. Their opposed faces have 0.7 static friction, 0.55 dynamic friction and 2 N configured jaw effort. The factory finger meshes are replaced, not overlaid. The original finger-link mass and inertia are retained as a conservative approximation. These replacement fingers and control limits apply to the demo and model. `pad_covers.py` supplies the geometry and physical closing stops.

## Material approximation

The tape is a chain of 72 rigid 15 mm paper panels with passive bend and twist joints. The native solver handles gravity, torso contact, self-contact and robot contact. Joint translation is locked. Broad-face bending is compliant (0.0016 N m/rad); torsion is compliant (0.00246 N m/rad); bending within the paper plane is much stiffer (16 N m/rad, ±5° per seam). This anisotropy prevents the strip behaving like a string when held upright. A broken joint or a seam opening above 3 mm invalidates the result. This is an approximation to a flexible, inextensible paper strip, not a calibrated model of a particular paper brand's creasing or tear strength.

A force-limited native joint approximates unresolved friction only after a paper panel lies between the closing finger faces, within a 0.6 mm native collision/contact tolerance per face. It starts at the observed pose, transmits reaction forces, releases on opening and breaks at 1.2 N or 0.012 Nm. The robot does not receive tape-to-form attachments. No execution code writes tape poses or moves objects to scoring targets. Printed ticks and digits follow measured panel poses and are visual only.

## Run

Use the pinned Isaac/YAM environment in `ENVIRONMENT.json`. In a fresh root-owned Ubuntu x86-64 GPU container with NVIDIA Vulkan support, `bash tasks/measure-chest-with-paper-tape/bootstrap.sh` installs it under `/workspace/robocurve-sim` (override with `ROBOCURVE_SIM_PREFIX`). Activate `IsaacLab-Arena/.venv` there. No other task folder is needed. Run from the repository root:

```sh
OMNI_KIT_ACCEPT_EULA=YES ACCEPT_EULA=Y python tasks/measure-chest-with-paper-tape/run.py --mode demo --out /absolute/new/demo-directory
OMNI_KIT_ACCEPT_EULA=YES ACCEPT_EULA=Y python tasks/measure-chest-with-paper-tape/run.py --mode dream --out /absolute/new/dream-directory
python tasks/measure-chest-with-paper-tape/rescore.py /absolute/run-directory
python tasks/measure-chest-with-paper-tape/score_checks.py
```

Each run freezes its sources, records native cameras and all body states, and saves `run.json` and `trace.jsonl`. Dream uses the official Inspect Robots bridge with eight actions per request to reduce idle expiry; run one model episode per API key. The normal policy cameras are unchanged. Extra judging cameras are recording-only. Sources use no random reset jitter. The current demo uses 780 controls (39 simulated seconds) and reaches full score. The matching Dream baseline completed all 1,600 controls (80 simulated seconds). On an RTX 6000 Ada, the demo took 12.2 minutes and the baseline 38.7 minutes including rendering and API latency. The baseline required 57 session-expiry recoveries: physical state continued, but model memory restarted. The full request log is retained; the low score cannot be attributed solely to task difficulty. The demo's unused policy module had a 48-action chunk default; the actual model baseline used 8, with identical physics, instruction, cameras and score. Earlier development baselines used different paper or finger geometry and are not submission evidence.

## Reset

Remove the tape, inspect it for tears or sharp permanent creases, and reuse it only if intact and easily flattened. Replace damaged strips with one from the same paper roll. Return it to the photographed loose, edge-standing curve. The torso and base remain fixed. This reset is expected to take a few minutes; no timed physical reset has been claimed.

## Scoring checks for a physical trial

Check the final state, including the last three seconds of video. Use front, back and side photos, an ordinary ruler, and the printed graduations. Measure the reference circumference once before trials with a flexible tape at the two chest landmarks; the supplied form is 88.4 cm there.

- A tear invalidates the result. In simulation a broken material joint or any seam separation above 3 mm is a tear. Penetration more than 3 mm into the rigid form also excludes credit.
- Follow the tape from its zero end around the form. A half-wrap is at least 180° around the chest; the higher partial score requires at least 300°. A continuous wrap must not turn back on itself. Small local waviness is allowed; the simulated angular-backtracking tolerance is 0.20 rad (11.5°).
- For partial credit, the strip centreline stays within 30 mm of the chest plane and its largest surface gap is at most 30 mm. Its width direction stays within 26° of vertical throughout the measuring portion.
- For full credit, the tape completes the loop with its zero end over the scale at the front. The largest centreline level error is 15 mm, the largest surface gap is 10 mm, and the zero-to-scale separation is at most 12 mm. Read the centimetre marks under the zero end: the result must be within 2 cm of the reference, continuously for three seconds.
- The unused tail after the overlap is not part of the measured loop. Judge the physical result, not which hand or route produced it. No force sensor, hidden material state, or precise tension target is required for physical scoring.

`model.py` evaluates these geometric checks from observed poses, using shortest distance to the native 64-sided chest surface for the gap check. `rescore.py` verifies the frozen sources and independently replays every recorded state and the three-second hold. `rescore_current.py` also audits any frozen run against the current geometric scorer while verifying that physics and policy inputs are unchanged. A numerical score still requires checking that the printed scale is visible in the recorded views.

### Paper stiffness estimate

The ribbon uses an assumed effective Young modulus of 1.8 GPa and Poisson ratio 0.3. Segment rotational stiffness follows EI/L for bending about each axis and GJ/L for torsion: 0.0016, 16.0 and 0.00246 N m/rad respectively. This preserves paper’s much greater resistance to bending within its own plane. These are engineering estimates, not measurements of this 160 gsm stock. Measured paper moduli of 1.69–3.98 GPa are reported in [Ramasubramanian et al., 2007](https://msp.org/jomms/2007/2-2/jomms-v2-n2-p01-s.pdf); humidity and fibre direction matter.
