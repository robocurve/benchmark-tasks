# Physical pen and cap specification

This specifies a physical counterpart to the simulated task. No fabricated
specimens, force measurements or physical qualification results are claimed.
All dimensions below are millimetres and use the pen/cap local x axis.

## Parts and materials

- Four marker bodies: pigmented polypropylene (PP), 19.4 outside barrel
  diameter, barrel interval x=−70 to +70. The shoulder, felt nib and tail have
  the external envelopes generated by `build_assets.py`, with 165.2 overall
  pen length. Use a hollow PP body containing a polyester felt reservoir/nib;
  adjust the enclosed reservoir or inert internal ballast to a total of
  35 ± 1 g. The mass includes every moving pen component. The simulated barrel
  is a solid collider, not a claim that the real barrel is solid PP.
- Four cap shells: PP, 35 overall length, approximately 32.6 maximum outside
  diameter, with the closed inside face at x=−14.5 and mouth at x=17.5.
  Inner flats are 25 apart, widening to a 25.8-across-flats seat from x=10
  to x=17.5. The assembled shell and silicone must total 8.0 ± 0.5 g. Use a
  ribbed/lightened shell rather than assuming the solid collision-wall volume
  has this mass: retain a continuous nominal 1 mm outside skin, the closed
  inside face, the liner seat and its key bosses, and remove material in
  enclosed pockets between the outer skin and inner support ribs. Finish the
  required inner contact envelope after fabrication. Weigh the shell and
  cured liner together; adjust enclosed relief/ballast without changing the
  mouth, seat, external grasp envelope or fit dimensions. These are mass and
  dimensional acceptance targets, not a validated manufacturing recipe.
- Four inserts: Smooth-On SORTA-Clear 40 platinum-cure silicone. Its
  [manufacturer specification](https://www.smooth-on.com/products/sorta-clear-40/)
  lists nominal Shore A 40 and density 1.08 g/cm³. Those material values do not
  establish a pen-retention force. Use the manufacturer's current processing
  instructions and verify full cure on the intended PP and tooling.
- Two destination markers: printed paper, 240 × 200, red left and blue right,
  attached flat to the existing rigid YAM tabletop. No raised container.

## Mechanically captured insert

The silicone has a 16-sided outer profile matching the 25.8-across-flats
recess. Its free bore is 19.0 diameter over x=10 to 16, widening linearly to
21.0 at x=17.5. The 19.4 barrel therefore produces nominal 0.2 radial
interference; the taper provides an entry lead. At the nominal final position
there is 3.9 of straight-liner overlap and 4.2 of nib-to-end clearance.

Provide four radial through-holes, 3.0 diameter, centered at cap x=13 on shell
flats at angles 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° around the cap axis. Each connects the
liner seat to the shell exterior. At the outside end, counterbore each hole
to 5.0 diameter by 0.6 deep. Keep full PP bosses around these holes when
lightening the shell. Cast the liner in place using a removable core defining
its free bore and entrance taper; let the same silicone fill all four holes
and counterbores. Finish each outer silicone head flush with its adjacent PP
flat, inside the existing external envelope. The resulting keys connect the
liner to four larger recessed heads and resist its axial movement through
mechanical interlock. No chemical adhesion to PP, primer or adhesive strength
is assumed. This replaces the earlier unspecified primer/adhesive design.

The key axes lie inside the straight-grip interval: their 3 mm axial span is
x=11.5 to 14.5. They are outside the free bore and do not alter its 19.0
diameter or obstruct the entrance. The keys, their counterbores and internal
shell weight-relief pockets are not modeled as separate collision features.
Their capture strength and the sleeve's friction still require physical
qualification; mechanical capture alone does not prove barrel retention.

## Qualification record for a physical trial

Use calipers, a scale and a force gauge, or a known hanging mass for the
static pull check. Record measured bore/barrel sizes, finished masses,
silicone batch/cure, shell construction and visible defects. The proposed
fit tolerance is 19.40 ± 0.05 for the barrel and 19.00 ± 0.05 for the free
liner bore, giving 0.15–0.25 radial interference before eccentricity.

Before using a set in a physical evaluation, check ten insertion/removal
cycles per pair. The chosen qualification targets are insertion force no
greater than 5 N, no liner/key migration greater than 0.2 mm, and retention
against a 0.5 N axial separation load for ten seconds after every cycle.
A 51 g hanging test mass supplies approximately 0.50 N, already greater than
the pen's approximately 0.34 N weight; record the complete suspended mass and
keep it clear of other supports. These are proposed fixture acceptance
criteria, not measured results or manufacturer guarantees. They are separate
from the task's scored one-second hand-clear retention/placement checks.
Revise the fixture and its evidence if it fails qualification.

## Simulation correspondence

The silicone's interfering inner zone is noncolliding visible geometry.
The rigid backing has a 21.2-across-flats aperture, and a pose-preserving
fixed joint is created only after the implemented geometric engagement and
seating-time checks. The joint approximates retention, with no calibrated
elastic response, insertion force or break force. It does not validate the
0.5 N physical test above. The nominal dimensions and all collision/mass
parameters remain those of the previous lined-cap simulation; the current
pen barrel visual is original analytic geometry. Simulation and physical
qualification are reported separately in the validation records.
