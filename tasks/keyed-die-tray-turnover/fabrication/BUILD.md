# Making the turnover fixture

All STL masters use millimetres. The physical jig is a custom bench trainer. Its working tray geometry comes from the passing simulation. The CAD adds physical shafts, bores, a detachable source stop and space for captive spring followers. A fabricator can inspect the meshes without Isaac Sim. Finish sliding faces, the pocket floors and the bores after printing or machining. The 0.05 mm nominal source side clearance merits a trial fit of both parts. An unprocessed desktop print at this clearance is an unreliable sliding fit.

## Assembly and commissioning

1. The hinge is the common X axis of the carrier masters. Install both knuckles and spacer sleeves on the main pivot. The two rest pads at Y = ±58 mm support the open carriers. Their tops are 6.66 mm below the pivot. Both carriers must swing freely through the upper half-turn and lie on a rest at either open endpoint. The handle's leverage about the hinge is 43 mm.
2. Insert the loaded-side tray under its side lips through the outboard end with the stop removed. Fit the stop with its inside face at Y = +67.5 mm, then secure the source tray before loading dies. Its underside has two Ø1.60 mm blind pilot bores at tray (X,Y) = (±19.6,0) mm, 2.30 mm deep from the underside. Bottom-tap M2 × 0.4 to leave at least 2.0 mm usable engagement. Deburr and check actual screw projection and thread engagement before tightening. The matching bed has Ø2.20 mm clearances at hinge-frame (±19.6,+42) mm with 90° Ø4.10 mm underside countersinks. Two M2 × 5 mm flat-head screws fit flush; confirm they hold the tray down without bottoming or marking its top. Keep the receiver free on the pedestal with its identical underside holes unused. Remove these screws before sliding the source tray out for service. The simulation represents the installed screws by a fixed joint between source tray and carrier. Put each tray on a surface plate and check that all four corners sit flat. The source retention slot nominally measures 50.90 mm across. Deburr or finish to a light sliding fit. The receiving slot measures 52.40 mm across the parallel guides. The two receiving lips run from 23.8 to 27.8 mm on either side of the centreline. At full nominal lateral play of 0.8 mm, the opposite lip still overlaps the flange by 0.8 mm and the near lip clears the raised central rim by 0.8 mm. The open apron and flared entry face outward. Insert the loose, correctly keyed tray by sliding it along the apron. It must stay under both guides when gently inverted in the closed jig.
3. Insert each plunger from the outside of the receiver. Its captive collar stops at the step from the 4.1 mm bore to the 3.0 mm guide. Add a spring then the M5 seat. From socket centre (X=50, Y=−55), measure the seat at 17.2 mm out on each side. The spring rests between collar and seat at 9 mm installed length. The guide axis lies 2.65 mm below the hinge plane. Fit a 1.6 mm diameter guide pin projecting 5.4 mm inward from the spring seat and use springs with free inside diameter at least 2.0 mm. The pin limits collar travel to 3.6 mm while the spring has room to compress. The finished pair must move freely and return to seat. Bind, excessive effort, or no positive retention calls for adjustment before loading coupons.
4. Bring the two leaves gently face to face, with empty trays. The striker must travel past the widest shoulder into the socket and remain engaged when turned as a pair. Use the two grips to reopen. Repeat with the tray and coupons after confirming clearances. For the proposed dry build, measure tangential closing and opening forces at the 43 mm handle radius with a spring scale. Record five cycles, including breakaway, return and any unintended release. An engineering target is ≤8 N on a single grip with the other supported. The projected cam motion and the force target are design checks; actual friction, reset repeatability and YAM pad traction require measurement.
5. Weigh the assembled moving parts before running the bench. The local material model targets 10.2 g per plain acetal tray, 50.5 g for the complete source carrier and 69 g for the receiver carrier including fixed metalwork before separate tray, rotating grip and moving followers. Each acetal grip is 6.55 g and each steel follower 0.372 g. Record deviations, then verify the centred and edge-of-slot handoffs. Damping, pad traction and the transfer rate still need bench calibration. The coupons are inert training material.
6. Place the hinge centre at world X=265, Y=0, Z=788 mm on the official station table at Z=747.5 mm. Local hinge X points along world +Y and local carrier Y along world −X. The pedestal is centred at world (379,−140) mm, top approximately Z=772.5 mm. Secure it without obstructing the tray edges. The empty tray's face datum starts at Z=776 mm, keyed +X/+Y corner rotated +90° about world Z. The loaded tray lies in the +local-Y holder; align its marked corner with its local +X/+Y corner. Take reset and final photographs with the clipped corner identified.

## Procurement and cost basis

- A US small-lot route uses one 100 mm diameter, 500 µm test silicon wafer. UniversityWafer lists ID 4201 at US$17.50. Ask a dicing service to deliver 9 mm finished squares, accounting for saw kerf. The price is for wafer stock. No functional device or custom wafer fabrication is required. [Vendor item](https://buy.universitywafer.com/products/100mm-silicon-wafer-id-4201)
- UT Austin publishes external assisted silicon dicing at US$94/hour and a US$10 external entry fee. Assuming one billed hour and one entry, silicon stock plus this stage totals **US$121.50**. Actual minimum, blade, mounting tape, marking, shipping, taxes and the number of service hours are unconfirmed. [Facility rates](https://tmi.utexas.edu/facilities/instrumentation/disco-321-wafer-dicing-saw)
- Black stock matching the tray color is listed as U.S. Plastic item 45433, 1/4 × 12 × 12 inch acetal. The sheet fits two trays. Its specific gravity is 1.41 and its listed regular price is US$69.65. The listing also showed a limited closeout, so use the regular price for planning. With the one-hour dicing example, the partial stock and service subtotal is **US$191.15**. Carrier manufacture, pins, springs, finishing, marking, freight and taxes are additional. The assembled fixture has no vendor quotation. [Black acetal sheet](https://www.usplastic.com/catalog/item.aspx?itemid=32870)

### Material estimates

The tray master with its two blind holes encloses 7.193 cm³. Acetal at 1.41 g/cm³ gives 10.14 g. The simulated tray is 10.2 g. The two small underside pilot bores and recessed screw heads are included in the manufacturing solids; the simulator represents the screw connection with the fixed joint and smooth exterior contact surfaces. The dry carrier estimates use aluminum at 2.70 g/cm³ and an allowance for the fixed shaft, stop screws and fittings. A 2.8 mm-nosed steel plunger of 0.0474 cm³ at 7.85 g/cm³ weighs 0.372 g. The individual components have not been weighed in hardware. Record the actual masses with the first build. [Inspection card](inspection-card-A4.pdf)
