# Mechanical commissioning record

Use the assembled inert water fixture. One operator supports the carriage while the other measures. Retain the original video and raw readings. These are acceptance targets for a new specimen; the repository has no physical spring-force, retention or robot-lifting measurements. Record the gauge ID, resolution, measured uncertainty and operator. A reading straddling a limit is inconclusive. Rework and repeat any failed group before a policy trial.

| Check | Method | Acceptance for commissioning |
|---|---|---|
| Location and fit | Set Z from table datum. Survey zero tab, brake tab, lift neck, sill, tap axis and three knob positions in the CAD coordinate system. With calipers inspect the three exposed gripping blocks and board relative to the station drawing. | Grip centers within 1 mm, contact lengths within 0.5 mm; shaft perpendicular to board within 1°. Quantity +100 mm sill-to-pointer within 0.5 mm. |
| Rack | Measure first and last tooth centers, then every center and its top elevation against common datum. Measure tongue/rack bearing width/overlap at hard return and clearance at full press. | Pitch 12.00 ±0.15; first-to-last span 204 ±0.4. Return overlap in X ≥5.0, width ≥14.5. Full-stroke rack clearance ≥2.0. |
| Guide rebate and rack bridges | Before mounting the board, use a feeler at the widest moving truck or end cap on the installed mast and sweep the full 180 mm. With the wet carriage supported and its pin clear, gauge each bridge where its swept path overlaps the rack; repeat after proof loading. Record the tool and smallest actual gap. | Square mast: at least 0.5 mm on each side of the widest moving block in the rebate. Both mast variants: at least 0.5 mm between the mast face and each overhanging side of the moving block, excluding the rail contact surfaces; free rolling across the stroke. Both bridges pass a 0.20 mm feeler without drag against the rack. |
| Guide and restraint | With an assistant taking the load, sweep the entire released guide, hoses filled. Inspect behind the board for chafe or rack collision. Measure hard end stops and midtravel lateral play. | Z limits 910 and 1090 ±1, lateral movement ≤0.5. At three heights and both directions the yoke is captive, spring returns unaided, and there is no sticking or hose tension that can carry the board. |
| Spring curve | Remove the spring and compress it between parallel seats on a force gauge at 18/16/14/12/10 mm. Then reinstall, remove tooth load, and repeat with gauge contact on the red tab along +X, while measuring housing-to-yoke gap. Take three cycles up and down. | Ideal spring alone 1.10/2.20/3.30/4.40/5.50 N ±10% (or ±0.15 N, whichever larger); installed force within 0.5 N of matching value. Total return-to-stop stroke 8.0 ±0.2 mm. A visible witness or ≤0.5 mm feeler establishes full return. |
| Breakaway and weight | Weigh the wet stationary-on-carriage group, movable brake group and rotating valve group separately. Tare the hanging hose at midtravel. Pull upward and lower slowly with a force gauge on the neck at three heights, pin fully clear. | Commission within ±5% of 400/45/25 g respectively; compare total to 470 g. Difference between mean upward lifting force and measured weight ≤1.0 N across the usable stroke (guide, hose drag). Signed downward/upward traces expose hose reactions. |
| Supported vs retained | At teeth n=4,14,16 independently support the board, seat the tongue, remove both supports, then hang **additional** mass equal to the measured total moving mass evenly from the board near the guide. Leave 60 s behind a low catch stop. Remove load before changing height. | About 0.94 kg combined mass at nominal target (about 9.2 N), pointer drift ≤0.5 mm, tongue still seated, no permanent bend or loose fastener. Witness alone is insufficient evidence. This is a trainer commissioning load. |
| Missed engagement | Take the weight manually. Press until ≥6 mm and move the tongue gently level with a tooth flank before allowing return. Observe that the witness stops short. Re-lift into the gap and repeat at low, middle and high teeth; then return with pin clear to test stops. | A blocked yoke cannot display engaged witness. At the gap it returns to ≤0.5 mm. With the hand removed over a catch, a correctly seated shelf carries the assembly. Ten successful cycles at each of three shelves. |
| Indexed targets and boundaries | Seat the released board at n=4,6,14,16. Survey zero relative to target datum and repeat on n±1 where within travel. | Target rests within ±1 mm of 939.75, 963.75, 1059.75, 1083.75 mm as appropriate. Adjacent 12 mm steps fall outside the 10 mm grade tolerance. |
| Tap | Paint endpoints from a verified square. Wet/prime, remove torsional hose load and swing the tap 10 times. Use a force gauge tangential at the measured 20 mm radius, torque = radius in m × force in N. Record release outcome at 0, 25, 35, 45, 55, 65 and 90° from both directions. | Stops 0 and 90 ±0.5°. Released end readings remain ≤2° or ≥88° for 30 s. On the proper side of the cam, 25° returns closed and 65° returns open; the exact onset and hysteresis are measured. Adjust ball preload and seal drag. Candidate torque window 0.006–0.035 Nm (0.3–1.75 N at 20 mm); final capability requires actual full-score manipulation with wet hoses. Check water endpoints and partial opening per WATER_CALIBRATION.md. |
| YAM feasibility | With catch support, use stock arms to reach and grip each real control at nominal starting and target heights. Run the full 60 s reference, record release, spindle angle and wet mass while viewing the entire operating interval. Stop a trial if clamp or support slips. | No guide assist, relocated grip, moving datum, hidden prop, continued support or replacement actuator. The independently retained board and graded flow during release are essential. Complete the physical score worksheet before reporting a physical pass. |

The linear drive in the simulator has stiffness 550 N/m, target −0.002 m, damping 3 N·s/m and positive travel 0–0.008 m. The physical spring and load path target the simulated static force curve. The installed-force and retention checks above establish whether a specimen meets those targets. Quasistatic calibration does not measure the numerical joint damping. The submitted valve model uses a clipped linear conductance curve and a capped angular drive with hysteretic switching. The manufactured plug, dry detent and hoses are tested in their assembled state. Material differences at intermediate angles must be recorded; a failed required water band invalidates commissioning. Separate a specimen or instrumentation fault from a failure induced by the robot, using the pre/post baseline checks.
