# Bench preparation and loaded dry run

Use a stationary robot bench, an inert mannequin and a passive water-training fixture. All fluid ports terminate inside the bench apparatus. Use smooth nonmedical plumbing and an open collector. Keep people outside the active robot workspace while it moves.

1. With power to the robot drives isolated, fit the catch tray and use a separate graduated jug to verify ≥750 mL usable capacity with the stands installed. Drain, dry and inspect for leaks. The largest fill permitted in an episode is 500 mL. Keep any refill jug elsewhere. Inspect and dry the tray between runs.
2. Anchor the fixed post/bench bracket, surrogate bed and supply before adding water. Put the filled reservoir in the documented secondary retention strap. Check its weighed, filled mass against the mount test. Keep power supplies, control boxes, mains cables, connectors and the operator outside the spill envelope. Use a separate battery scale with a splash roof/clear lip and a level pan, its display visible. Avoid any mechanical bypass of the weighing pan.
3. Prime with the arms parked and drives isolated. The open discharge lip, vented transfer and receiver air gap must remain visible. Confirm the maximum fault-flow test stays inside the tray. Close the tap and drain the test water before restoring robot power.
4. First operate by hand on the unpowered trainer. Weigh the filled moving assembly with hose in its marked operating loop. Measure upward/downward hose reaction and pull resistance per the mechanical procedure. Using a payload dummy equal to the measured mass, execute the raised and descending paths with drives enabled at the station's reduced-speed setup setting and an attendant at the emergency stop. Start with dry plumbing, then repeat with the commissioned fill. Never use a person as a surrogate.
5. In each trial inspect the full jaw/arm envelope and wrist cameras at start, grasp, fully withdrawn brake, loaded travel, seating, 90° tap sweep and retreat. Sweep the moving hose through both end stops by hand at setup. Confirm the gripper brackets, clips, tube, forearms, sight, tray rim and scale remain clear. Compare the settled zero before/after removing support. Record any binding, stall, spill, slippage or unexpected contact. Physical loaded success requires the actual unit to pass these checks.
6. If water leaves the tray or reaches electrical equipment, interrupt motion with the station stop, isolate equipment per the laboratory shutdown procedure and recover the apparatus before resetting. A leak, fixture or recording failure independent of policy invalidates that trial. A robot-caused kink, broken seal, tipped vessel or moved fixture is documented as a scored fault. Store the original video and timestamp before cleaning/resetting.
