Open the wooden drawer fully. First, move the left gripper to the blue drawer handle. Position the fingers on opposite sides of the handle, close the gripper around it, and pull the handle outward toward the robot bases. Keep the right arm away while the left arm grasps and begins pulling. If the drawer stops because the orange cloth is caught, keep the left gripper closed on the handle and pause pulling. Bring the right gripper to the exposed orange cloth and press it downward into the drawer. While the right gripper maintains downward pressure, continue pulling the handle with the left arm. Once the cloth clears the cabinet edge, lift the right gripper away and finish pulling the drawer fully open.
