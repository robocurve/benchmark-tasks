# Pop open a flat-folded 2-cup drink carrier and load two lidded hot cups onto the pickup mat

Instruction: `Open the flat 2-cup drink carrier and seat both lidded hot cups upright in its pockets before it crosses the white tape line, then leave the loaded carrier standing on the blue pickup mat.`

## Who does this and why it matters (Impact on jobs)

- **Occupations:** Fast Food and Counter Workers (O*NET `35-3023.00`; **3,676,580** US workers, BLS OEWS May 2023; Core task: *"Serve customers in eating places that specialize in fast service and inexpensive carry-out food"*) and Cashiers (O*NET `41-2011.00`; **3,298,660** US workers, BLS OEWS May 2023; Core task: *"Bag, box, wrap, or gift-wrap merchandise"*), spanning supermarket coffee/juice bars, bakery-cafes, convenience stores, and takeout front counters.
- **Why two hands in distinct roles are physically required:**
  1. **Popping the flat-packed carrier open against crease return (`>= 13..21 cm` wrist separation):** `Southern Champion Tray (SCT) 2795` 2-cup handled kraft carriers stand flat-folded (`4.2 cm` folded width) in a counter U-slot rack (`(19.0, 0.0)`), whose `4.4 cm` slot physically prevents opening in the rack. Once lifted onto the counter, the `1.20 N` paperboard gusset return spring exceeds the `~0.10 N` sliding friction of the empty `42 g` carrier on the oak-laminate counter (`mu = 0.24`), so pulling one pocket collar with a single hand merely drags the folded carrier without expanding the pocket. One hand must anchor the `6.6 cm` top handle arch / opposite pocket collar while the other hand pulls each `5.6 cm` end-wall collar outward past `9.5 cm` so both hinged `9.0 x 8.0 cm` auto-bottom floor flaps swing flat onto their `+-90 deg` stops and mechanically prop the `9.5 mm` bottom ledges open (`~9.15 cm` per pocket).
  2. **Cross-counter drink staging and steadied pocket insertion (`>= 15.8 cm` wrist separation):** Both `245 g` `Solo 378W` `8 oz` lidded hot cups start on the right-side drink pass (`y = -24.0 cm`, reachable by the right arm), whereas the customer pickup mat sits on the left counter (`y = +22.0 cm`, reachable by the left arm). One hand steadies the light empty `42 g` carrier by its opposite collar while the other hand lowers the first tapered `8.0 cm`-rim cup through its `8.4 cm`-tall pocket collar onto the pocket floor (only `~4 mm` rim clearance per side); the second cup then goes into the far pocket of the cup-weighted carrier (`cup_2` is handed across the midline so each arm loads its near-side pocket).

## Scoring

Evaluated at trial end (`SCORE_OVER = "end"`), with only off-table drops, cup spills, damage and the loading-zone order latched across the trial (empty-carrier tilt during extraction/unfolding and final empty-gripper jaw opening are unrestricted):

- **Score 0** (latched over the whole trial) if at any time:
  - any carrier panel or either lidded cup leaves the `110 x 130 cm` station tabletop (`center z < 0`, center outside `x in [-19, 91] cm`, center `|y| > 65 cm`, or lowest point `< -3.0 cm`);
  - either lidded cup tilts `> 25.0 deg` from vertical (drink spill);
  - **damage:** any single robot link (finger pad, finger, wrist or arm link) presses on either lidded cup with `> 20.0 N` or on any carrier panel with `> 30.0 N` PhysX normal contact force (checked every control step). Cup limit: a conventional paper hot-cup sidewall already deflects `6.35 mm` (`0.25 in`) under only `~500 g = 4.9 N` (US Patent `5,772,111` sidewall-rigidity test), so `20.0 N` (`~4x`) creases the wall or pops the `TL38R2` lid. Carrier limit: `~310 g/m^2` kraft carton board buckles at `~6.5 kN/m` short-span compression (CD; Nordic Pulp & Paper Research Journal 2023, doi `10.1515/npprj-2023-0021`), i.e. `~30 N` over a `~5 mm` edge contact, which creases or tears the collar, divider or handle.
- **End-state tiers** (requires clear release—neither gripper holding any object and both gripper TCPs `>= 2.0 cm` clear of the carrier and both cups—and the carrier popped open upright `>= 2.0 cm` clear of the rack: both pocket spans `>= 8.2 cm` from central divider to end-wall center along carrier `y`, both auto-bottom floors `<= 12.0 deg` from horizontal, carrier tilt `<= 8.0 deg`, and carrier bottom `<= 1.0 cm` above mat/table; any non-qualifying end state scores **`0.00`**):
  - **`0.25`**: `0` cups seated, with the carrier popped open upright `>= 2.0 cm` clear of the rack and released (`TCPs >= 2.0 cm`).
  - **`0.50`**: `0.25` plus **1** lidded cup seated upright (`cup tilt <= 8.0 deg`, `cup base <= 1.5 cm` above carrier bottom, XY center within `2.2 cm` of a pocket center).
  - **`0.70`**: `0.25` plus **both** lidded cups seated upright in the two separate pockets (`L` and `R`), not yet standing inside the blue pickup mat.
  - **`0.85`**: `0.70` plus the carrier's footprint (`central spine` and both `end walls`, with `2.0 mm` boundary tolerance) standing completely inside the `16.0 x 24.0 cm` blue pickup mat at `(32.0, +22.0)`, **and** the carrier was loaded on the counter side of the tape line: both cups were seated (same seated test as above) while the carrier spine center was at `y < +8.0 cm`, before the spine center first reached `y >= +8.0 cm` (latched; an empty or 1-cup carrier that crosses the line first stays capped at `0.70`, so carrying the empty carrier to the mat and loading it there does not count).
  - **`1.00`**: `0.85` plus both gripper TCPs parked `>= 10.0 cm` clear of the carrier and both cups, moving `< 2.0 cm/s` continuously over the final `1.0 s`.

**Real-trial verification:** Overhead and side video plus end-state ruler and digital-inclinometer measurements verify zero off-table drops (no carrier or cup center outside `x in [-19, 91] cm` or beyond `|y| > 65 cm`, below the tabletop, or lowest point `> 3.0 cm` below it) and zero `> 25.0 deg` cup tilt across the trial; a post-trial damage inspection (both cups and lids, all carrier panels, under bright raking light) finds no creased, dented or crushed cup wall, no lid unseated from the rim, and no torn, creased-through or buckled carrier collar, divider, gusset or handle (the YAM gripper current limit is set so each pad squeezes `<= 20.0 N` on cups and `<= 30.0 N` on the handle, matching the sim limits); clear gripper release (`>= 2.0 cm`); both pocket spans `>= 8.2 cm` (divider midplane to end-wall center, ruler across pocket top) with auto-bottom floors flat (`<= 12.0 deg`) and carrier upright (`<= 8.0 deg`, bottom `<= 1.0 cm` above mat/table, `>= 2.0 cm` from rack); both cups seated upright (`<= 8.0 deg` tilt, cup base `<= 1.5 cm` above carrier bottom, XY center within `2.2 cm` of pocket center); overhead video showing both cups seated before the carrier's central divider first crosses the taped `y = +8.0 cm` line (line extended with a straightedge across the overhead frame); full carrier footprint within the `16.0 x 24.0 cm` blue mat (`2.0 mm` tolerance); and `>= 10.0 cm` TCP clearance from the carrier and cups held `< 2.0 cm/s` over the final `1.0 s`.

## Simulation fidelity

- **5-body articulated `SCT 2795` carrier (`42 g` total, `240 Hz` PhysX):** `16 g` central `2-ply` handle spine, two `6 g` end walls on `Y`-axis prismatic gusset joints (`0..8.3 cm` travel, `1.20 N` crease return spring), and two `7 g` `9.0 x 8.0 cm` `X`-revolute auto-bottom floor panels (`0..+-90 deg` hard stops, `0.0025 N m/deg` crease bias capped at `0.018 N m`) that collide physically with `9.5 mm` inward end-wall bottom tabs—staying blocked upright while folded, then dropping flat at `> 9.5 cm` pull to prop the pockets locked open at `~9.15 cm` with zero proximity latches.
- **Tapered weighted cups:** Each `245 g` `Solo 378W` cup + `TL38R2` traveler dome lid uses a 5-slice tapered cylinder (`5.1 cm` base to `8.0 cm` rim) plus lid skirt and dome (`CoM 4.2 cm` above base).
- **Force-limited two-pad grip (`grip_limited.py`, sim stand-in for elastomer pad friction; no rigid weld):** A compliant 6-axis spring-damper joint (`20 kN/m`, `50 N m/rad`) anchored at the current relative pose (no snap) engages **only** when both finger pads (`tip_left` and `tip_right`) maintain verified PhysX normal squeeze force on opposite sides of the target (`>= 3.0 N` per pad on cups, the `5 mm` collar boards, and the `6.6 cm` handle arch—exceeding the `2.40 N` cup weight and `1.20 N` gusset spring; `>= 5.5 N` per pad on the `9 mm` central handle bar—exceeding the `5.22 N` loaded carrier weight) while the jaws are commanded closed and stalled on the part. The YAM `i2rt linear_4310` gripper can squeeze up to `50.0 N` per finger (`yam_arena` `GRIPPER` effort), but the damage rule bounds the usable squeeze to `20.0 N` per pad on cups, collars and the `6.6 cm` arch and `30.0 N` on the rolled handle rim, so every axis is force-capped from those squeezes, rubber pad friction `mu = 0.5`, and a `~20 x 30 mm` pad patch: **`20.0 N` pull** (`2 pads x 0.5 x 20.0 N`; `30.0 N` on the rim), **`0.20 N m` twist** about the pad normal (`20.0 N x 10 mm` mean patch radius), and **`0.60 N m` in-plane tipping** (`2 x 20.0 N x 15 mm` half pad length) on cups, collars, and the `6.6 cm` outer handle pillars, or **`0.027 N m` roll/tipping** (`2 pads x mu_rim 0.10 x 30.0 N x 4.5 mm` bar radius) when pinching the smooth `9 mm` cylindrical central handle rim along `y`. The scripted policy closes its jaws only a few millimetres past first pad contact (`~10 N` per pad on cups and collars, `~18 N` on the handle rim) instead of driving them to the `50 N` effort limit. Above any cap the object slips in the jaws (anchor moves and logs); opening the jaws or losing two-pad squeeze for `5` steps (`0.25 s`) releases the grip immediately. Paperboard tearing and cup denting are not simulated as deformation; they are scored through the contact-force damage limits above. Liquid slosh is not simulated.
- **Floor-flap compliance stand-in:** the two `3 mm` auto-bottom floor flaps are rigid in sim, so the finger links do not collide with those two flap bodies (a real folded paperboard flap bends aside under a fingertip); fingers still collide with the end walls, collars, divider, handle, cups, rack, mat and table, and the flaps still collide with the end walls, cups and table, so the auto-bottom lock and cup seating are fully physical.
