# Asset provenance

All task-specific USD geometry and the dimension drawing are original parametric constructions included with this task. `build_assets.py` regenerates the USD assets and mass ledger. Product pages are dimensional, material and mass references, not sources of copied product meshes, logos or artwork. Reference values and design assumptions are distinguished in `PHYSICAL_DESIGN.md` and `physical_design.json`.

The unchanged station, robot meshes, table appearance and cameras come from the installed YAM Arena environment under its own upstream licenses. They are not redistributed here. No proprietary product CAD or scanned mesh is included.
