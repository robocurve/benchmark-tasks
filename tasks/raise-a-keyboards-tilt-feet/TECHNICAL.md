# Physical reconstruction and independent grading

The original generated CAD has a 450 × 155 × 23.6 mm keyboard envelope, 543 g
main body and two 3.5 g feet. Each rear foot is 24 × 15 × 2.4 mm with a molded
lift lip and a passive Y-axis revolute hinge, limited to 0 through −120 degrees.
The front rubber pads are 16 × 25 × 4 mm. Underside recesses admit the feet's
actual swept volume. Adjacent hinge-body collision is enabled so the real elastic friction cheeks
contact the molded ears. Keyboard, feet, desk, notebook and robot all use native contact. The deployed foot extends past vertical, with its toe behind the hinge. An upward desk reaction therefore loads the native open hard stop. The elastic cheeks retain a foot while unloaded; friction is not asked to carry the operating load. The 120° stop and 24 mm leg are original generic engineering geometry, not measurements of a K270 mechanism.

The shell, keys and rubber regions have separate original analytic colliders.
Keys are fixed geometry; key travel and typing are outside this operation.
Masses, lumped inertias, center of mass, friction and detail geometry are
engineering estimates. No molded snap-detent force or plastic deformation
calibration is claimed. This generic full-size model is not an OEM replica.



## Physical grading

Start underside-up. Score 0.3 when one rear foot has opened past 110 degrees from folded, 0.6 when both have, and 0 otherwise. Full credit requires both still open past 115 degrees after turnover. With keys up and both hands and the notebook clear, the keyboard must sit entirely on the desk, tilted 4–14 degrees front to back. Both rear toes and both front pads must be within 1 mm of the desk. View each rear corner separately against a 1 mm reference, then confirm the two front pads. Hold this released pose for 1 s without sliding more than 2 mm from the start of the hold or rotating more than 2 degrees. A folded-under foot or a visible gap under one support caps the score at 0.6. Falling off the desk voids the trial.
