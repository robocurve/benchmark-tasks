# inline-skate-ratchet-cuff-buckle build deviations

1. **Import order & dynamic unbolted cuff assembly**: Added `import mechkit` before `import labkit` (preventing import-finder bypass) and converted `cuff` from `fixed=True` to a dynamic `0.260 kg` free shell assembly (`0.454 kg` total with hinged links) resting unbolted on a fixed rubber `bench_pad`. Pushing the `6 N` sprung cuff ear from one side without pinching/counter-holding slides/rotates the cuff off the pad; internal two-ear pinching by the left arm (`ANCHOR_POS` + `WTAB_POS`) stabilizes the cuff with zero net lateral drift.
2. **Mirrored side assignment & 16 mm strap tab clearance**: Placed the squeeze ear pair on `+Y` (`left` arm) and the ratchet buckle, lever, and `42 g` ladder strap (`>= 35 g`) on `-Y` (`right` arm), placing the pinch tab `TAB_AT = 16 mm` from the rear strap rivet (`>= 38 mm` clear of the buckle receiver mouth) and adding `+-15 deg` wrist splay (`_splay`) so `left_link4` and `right_link4` maintain zero arm-arm contact (`0 N`).
3. **`hinge_turn` circular lever arc**: Replaced vertical linear lever waypoints with `pitch_demo.hinge_turn` about the world `LEVER_AXIS` and enlarged the lever knob to `24 x 18 x 20 mm` (`0.032 kg >= 25 g`), achieving three crisp `~42 deg` ratchet strokes (`3` notches) across all `0.8x / 1.25x / 1.5x` friction and `solref=0.011` cases.
4. **Coupled ratchet mechanism (`SelfCollisions=False`)**: Because `labkit` authors one articulation with `SelfCollisions=False`, ratchet pawl locking is implemented cleanly via `_rules` + `_drives` (`K_LOCK = 25 N*m/rad` holding `wing <= hold_angle(notches)`) rather than fragile sub-mm intra-articulation teeth collision.
