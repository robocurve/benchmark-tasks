# Building the props

All items are ordinary purchases except the cradle chocks and the sign; no drawings needed beyond these sizes.

| Prop | Size / material | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Cart top | stainless-steel shelf or sheet, 38 x 84 cm, 2 cm thick (or a stainless cart top), on the bench; red tape band 3 cm wide across the front edge | 40-80 USD |
| Sign | yellow board 36 x 20 cm on a stand, red band, text "MR Zone IV ahead", printed | 10 USD |
| Tray | red plastic tray 20 x 72 cm, 4 cm walls (or a bin of that size) | 15 USD |
| Oxygen cylinder | empty steel M6 / B-size cylinder, 6.4 cm diameter, about 29 cm with valve, about 1.3 kg (valve closed and capped; any empty steel cylinder of this size, e.g. a retired medical one) | 40-100 USD |
| Cradle chocks | four 1.4 x 2 x 2 cm plastic or wood blocks glued to the shelf, two on each side of the cylinder at 6.5 cm either side of its middle | 1 USD |
| Trauma shears | steel-bladed, 19 cm, orange plastic handles | 8 USD |
| Smartphone | any phone in a case, about 15.5 x 7.8 x 1.3 cm, about 0.2 kg | any |
| Water bottle | 500 ml plastic bottle, 6.6 cm diameter, 20 cm tall, filled | 1 USD |
| Clipboard | plastic clipboard 23 x 31 cm with paper chart | 5 USD |
| Emesis basin | plastic kidney dish, about 11 x 20 cm | 2 USD |

Place everything as in `task.json` (setup). Only steel items must be steel; do not use a magnet or an MRI.
