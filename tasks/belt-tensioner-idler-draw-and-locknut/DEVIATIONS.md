# Geometry and physics deviations from benchtop assembly hardware

1. **Dual-joint clamp collar**: A real M8 hex/knurled locknut advances helically via screw threads; in rigid-body simulation the collar uses a cylindrical stack coupling a `hinge` (`spin`) with a 4 mm linear `clamp` `slide` joint advancing at 1.0 mm per 180 deg turn (2.0 mm/rev). `clamp_seated(spin, adv)` enforces both minimum angular travel (`>= 180 deg`) and axial closure within `0.2 mm` of the 1.0 mm stud gap.
2. **Rule-driven gauge stop and clamp lock**: `_rules()` modulates the `draw` slide drive stiffness to `K_STOP = 2600 N/m` at `SHIM_STOP = 21 mm` while `setup_gauge` sits in its frame pocket and to `K_CLAMP = 5200 N/m` once `clamp_seated(spin, adv)` holds.
3. **Softened belt return spring**: Belt tensioners typically use 8-12 N preload; here the carriage `draw` slide uses `K_SPRING = 160 N/m` (`3.36 N` at `SHIM_STOP = 21 mm`) so one articulated gripper arm can draw the carriage cleanly against arm PD compliance without lifting the unbolted 360 g sub-frame.
4. **Ergonomic grip interfaces**: A knurled collar T-wing bar (`40 x 18 x 22 mm`), X-pinch draw grip block (`20 x 28 x 36 mm` with steel guard flanges), and 28 x 36 mm gauge top cap lip replace bare cylindrical bosses so parallel-jaw silicone finger pads retain positive form closure during draw and two-stroke fastening.
5. **Expanded gauge pocket clearance**: Left rail is offset outward (`y = 0.148`) and the gauge pocket walls stand 22 mm tall with an `11 mm` inner channel around the `6 mm` acetal stem so the setup gauge seats flat on the deck without riding up under right-arm tightening torque.
