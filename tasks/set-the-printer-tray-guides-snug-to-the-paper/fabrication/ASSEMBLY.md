# FC1 assembly and machining authority

The general profile tolerance is ±0.10 mm, thickness ±0.05, hole position ±0.05. Running bores, tooth phase, axial floats and stated clearance dimensions take precedence. Deburr R0.3 maximum; keep the datum faces straight. Target pad flatness ≤0.15, carriage-induced pad skew ≤0.10. These are shop and acceptance requirements, not achieved measurements. Every fastener is A2 stainless unless specified. Metric threads coarse: M1.6×0.35, M2×0.4, M3×0.5, M4×0.7. Machined threads in plastics require at least 1.5 thread diameters engagement. Use removable threadlocker on metal-only joints; no lubricant on paper-contact faces.

## Floor and table fixing

Floor 304×417×3, origin U−152, V−12. Front panel V−12..0, Z0..65; side inner faces U±146; rear inner face V396. Plinth sides thickness 6 at U±146..152, Z−35..0; ends V−12..−6 and 399..405. The bench closes the bottom; no extra bottom skin. The existing connector slots remain real cutouts. Rod slots are near U−136..−62, V138..142 and rear U−2..2, V229..383. They clear the Ø2 rods by ≥1 mm, including the 0.0865 mm crank lateral shift. Do not add a cover across the moving rod path. Floor slots lie behind paper-contact faces and are not measurement rulers.

Four internal table anchors sit U134..146 mirrored, V6..18 and 384..396, Z−35..−23. Their L base is 3 thick. Two M3×9 countersunk screws per anchor pass horizontally through each 6 mm plinth side and engage 3 mm in its upright; heads flush at U±152. Trim and deburr to 9 mm. Each base uses one M4×8 screw into a tapped M4 bench or metal insert with ≥5 mm thread engagement. Screw axes U±138, V12/390; base hole Ø4.5, floor service opening Ø8 directly above. Remove flush PET service plugs only during setup. Screw heads are below Z−28 and outside all moving mechanisms. Table preparation is a four-hole mounting requirement, not a claim about the supplied table. Use the same fixed datum for both lots. Tighten M4 to 1.5 N m into metal inserts; test fixture movement <0.2 mm under 20 N in both horizontal directions. The hold test is unperformed.

Plinth-to-floor and panel-to-floor joints use machined aluminium 10×10×3 angle strips at the wall corners, M3×8 at 50 mm maximum pitch, edge distance ≥5. Drill mating 3.2 clearance and M3 tapped holes in a square assembly jig. Side/end corners use the same angle and two M3 per leg at Z−27/−8. These are fixed shell attachments; no head projects into the paper opening. Initial torque 0.5 N m for Al threads, 0.25 N m into POM; inspect and retighten before calibration.

## Reset and trial

Width free pad faces U±109, V90..190; rear face V356, U±70. Width carriages each travel inward 70, rear toward feed 150. Carriage stops are the shaft washers and split collars in AXLES.md. Check endpoints against 0/70 and 0/150 travel gauges, acceptance ±0.10. Initial reset phases the rack against 218 mm and 356 mm gauges. Inspect all travel manually before loading paper.

Use four unbound groups of 25 loose A4 sheets. No glue, rigid plates or handles. Use the adjacent lot table for fanning/offset/yaw; remove setup gauges before trial. Record sheet mass and thickness. The mechanism is custom and paper bowing/friction equivalence is uncalibrated. Full-score boundaries remain pad compression ≤2 mm, gap ≤1 mm and the task's angle, support, hand-clearance and stillness limits. A readout uncertainty that crosses 2.0 mm is indeterminate and needs a finer displacement measurement; do not silently change the boundary.


For the near and rear latched backplates only, mill a top-open relief through N−22..−18 across S=latch centre±31, Z27.5..36. The far backplate stays full height. This clears the bridge foot, full crank sweep, axle and collars; pin bores and end clamp holes remain outside the relief. Two 0.5 mm shims at N−22..−18, S=latch centre±10, Z27.5..28 support the bridge. Two vertical M2×8 countersunk screws at N−20, S±10 have heads flush Z32 and tips Z24, engaging 3.5 mm in blind 4-deep backplate threads from Z27.5. Do not retain a full-height solid backplate behind the hinge.
