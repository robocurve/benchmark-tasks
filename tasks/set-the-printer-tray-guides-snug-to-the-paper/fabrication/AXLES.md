# FC1 rails, width coupling and drag

All running shafts are ground stainless Ø6 h6, straightness ≤0.05/300, Ra ≤0.4. Width shafts U−145..145, length 290, V100/180, Z−11. Rear shafts V203..399, length 196, U±60, Z−25. Width plain blocks 20 along U × 12 across V × 12 high, bore Ø6.10 along travel at local B6, C6; top −5, bottom −17. Rear plain block same, top −19, bottom −31. One near-width and one rear block are replaced by 14 high split drag blocks with bore at C6; tops −3/−17, bottoms unchanged. The other four run freely.

Width shaft supports are 4 along shaft, 16×16 cross-section, bore Ø6.00 H7 at B8, C8. Position U141..145 mirrored, V axis ±8, Z−19..−3. One 1 mm spacer to side wall inner U±146. Two M2×10 countersunk from outside wall U±152 at B3, C3/10.5 engage 3 mm in support; head flush. Split 1 from top to shaft bore at B8. Clamp M3 across B at A2, C13.5 (one half Ø3.2, opposite tapped M3), tighten 0.15 N m then confirm shaft fixed. Clamp bore/screw gap 0.9, top wall 0.9. Carriage outermost reset ends U±139; supports inner faces ±141 leave 2 mm for reset stop washers.

Rear supports 12 along shaft, same 16×16 cross section, centres V209/393. Front spans 203..215, rear 387..399. At front, 3 mm Al hanger lies V200..203, Z−33..0, U axis ±10, upper 8 mm flange V192..200 below floor. Two M3×10 countersunk through floor at U axis ±6, V196 fasten with nuts below the flange. The 3 mm floor plus 3 mm flange plus 2.4 mm nut leaves 1.6 mm tip projection, ending Z−7; this is 20 mm ahead of the carriage sweep and clear of the shaft. Two M2×8 along V at B3, C3/10.5 fasten support. Rear support bolts directly through 6 mm plinth end with M2×10 countersunk flush V405. Bore/clamp as width but A6. Carriage centres 226..376, 20 long, envelopes 216..386: 1 mm between block and support at endpoints. Table clearance support bottom −33 vs −35 is 2 mm. No projecting screw below Z−33.

Travel stops are removable shaft hardware: four width reset washers OD10/ID6.2×2 occupy U−141..−139 and 139..141 on both width shafts. Four split stop collars OD14/ID6.1×4 occupy U−49..−45 and 45..49 on both width shafts; their inner faces set 70 mm stroke. Cross clamp M2 at axial 2, C5.5, split 1 from bore top to OD, 0.10 N m. Collar/screw bore ligament ≥1.35; top wall 0.4. Rear uses four 1 mm washers OD10/ID6.2 at V215..216 and 386..387 on both rear shafts. End contact is deliberate; no pad is used as a carriage stop. Verify endpoint position ±0.10 with gauges. Select stop shims only, never change tooth phase to hide a stop error.

Opposed coupling racks: module 1, 20° pressure angle, length 200, authored profile 6 high×6 face; pitch line V130 lower/150 upper. Near rack at reset U−139.80..60.20, teeth toward positive V; far U−61.26..138.74, teeth toward negative V. Both occupy Z−9..−3. These origins are −44.5π/−19.5π; both have a tooth valley at U0 when pinion tooth centres point along U/V. Nominal pinion flank relief gives 0.04 mm backlash per mesh. Each follows its moving beam, so at 70 stroke extents remain U±140 and clear side walls U±146 by 6 mm. Mount near at U−136/−132, V126.5; far at U132/136, V153.5. Two 1 mm aluminium tubular spacers OD4/ID2.2 between rack top −3 and beam underside −2, M2×6 countersunk flush beam top 0 into 3 mm blind rack threads. Beam slots 2.2×3.2 along U provide phase adjustment ±.5. Required backlash ≤0.20 over 70 stroke is an acceptance target, not measured. Use contact marking to inspect root clearance, deburr, and reject binding. Cutter radius ≤.25 allowed only in radial root transitions below base circle; no contact flank modification.

Pinion is a free idler, not fixed to axle: 20 teeth, module 1, 20°; authored profile OD22, pitch dia 20, bore 6.10 +.03/−0, face 5.80±.03. Centre U0, V140. POM gear actual faces Z−8.9..−3.1. Custom fixed stainless axle: integral top shoulder Ø10, Z−2.5..0; bearing journal Ø6 h6, Z−9.5..−2.5. Top axial M3 blind 4 from Z0; bottom axial M3 blind 4 from Z−9.5. A M3×7 countersunk screw from floor top Z3 fixes shoulder against floor underside 0. Top thrust washer OD10/ID6.1×.5 occupies −3..−2.5. Lower washer same −9.5..−9, retained by OD10×.5 washer and M3×5 screw from below, head lowest −12.5. Retaining screw engages 4 mm in axle and is threadlocked; no radial set screw in gear. Gap 6.00±.05 minus gear 5.80±.03 gives axial float 0.12..0.28. Axle fixed, gear free; inspect meshing and free spin before coupling carriages.

Drag block A0..20, B0..12, C0..14: Ø6.10 shaft bore along A at B6, C6; 1 mm split B6, C14 down to bore; M3 cross screw along B at A14, C11.5, Ø3.2 clearance half/opposite M3 tap, washer+jam nut. Minimum ligament 0.85 to shaft bore, top wall 0.9. Adjust loose first to 0.60±0.10 N at 20 mm/s with force gauge, then measure breakaway and 20/50/100 mm/s both directions. This is dry friction; a single 0.60 N target is not a 30 N s/m damper. Record actual curve and temperature. No physical coefficients are claimed.
