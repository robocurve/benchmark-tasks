Record instrument identity, resolution, uncertainty, temperature, operator, and actual result. The blank `calibration-record.csv` is not evidence of completed tests. Reject a component or assembly that misses the drawing; do not change the task boundary or label zero to conceal error.

1. Inspect floor/feed edge squareness ≤0.2 over 210 mm, floor flatness ≤0.2, pad face flatness ≤0.15, actual hole fits, all countersinks/nut pockets and moving contact envelopes. Move every carriage across full stroke and every latch 0..0.17 with pads 0/2/5. Check minimum actual clearances HINGE.md. Check stop washers/collars, guide backup stops and pin retention. No fixed carrier face may bypass the sprung pad.
2. Set zero stops with unloaded pad face N0; shim spacers only. Initial total S1 force ≤0.15 N; no intentional preload. Set paired compression stops at 5.000±.05. With dial indicator resolution ≤.02 and force gauge resolution ≤.02 N, measure each pad at 0, .5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5 loading/unloading, centre and both ends. Target pair rate 1.500±.075 N/mm, skew ≤.10, compression/release hysteresis ≤.10 N over 0..2. Select springs to meet rate; record rejects. Predicted forces are 0, 1.5, 3, 4.5, 6, 7.5 N at 0/1/2/3/4/5, not measurements.
3. Set scales per SCALE.md and record index errors separately for each pad. A 2.0±uncertainty reading that spans the decision boundary is indeterminate; use finer measurement, keep 2.0 unchanged.
4. Set pawl pickup gap 0.45 ±0.02 by feeler/displacement, calibrate actual release. Locked below .09 rad and free by .11, ≥.05 tip clearance by .115. Confirm 0/.17 angular stops. Phase at rack by reset gauges and inspect full tooth engagement; off-phase release must remain unseated. Test 10 N both directions for 10 s, drift ≤.2 mm. Do not claim 400 N capacity. Measure assembled handle force at 26 mm height above pivot, including S2, pawl return, drag and bearing friction. Target spring-only 1.54/2.62 N at .10/.17; added reaction is measured separately, no assertion of exact model matching.
5. With latch fully squeezed, adjust one width/one rear drag bush to 0.60±.10 N at 20 mm/s. Record breakaway and 20/50/100 mm/s both directions over full stroke, actual temperature, and any binding. It is dry friction. Inspect width mirror backlash ≤.20 at 0/35/70, and guide face parallelism ≤.20 across pad length. Weigh finished moving assemblies, including racks, rods, pawls, beams, anchors and fasteners. Replace calculated mass estimates only with recorded measurements.
6. Mount with four bench anchors; fixture movement <.2 under 20 N. Set width free faces 218 apart and rear V356, confirm teeth seated and pad pointers zero. Set both lot paper states using removable gauges, then remove gauges. Confirm loose paper—not glued stacks—and manual squeeze/slide/square/ease/release remains possible. Record paper thickness/mass and material condition. No paper-bowing law is validated by matching 3 N.

Tolerance allocation targets: pad face/carry alignment 0.15, mirror backlash 0.20, locked drift 0.20, readout error 0.10. Their worst-case arithmetic sum 0.65 is below 1 mm gap but is not a certification: measure the assembled gap and compression independently. A 0.10 compression readout error cannot reliably decide 1.95 versus 2.05; use finer measurement at boundary. Keep all manufacturing targets and numerical model approximations visible in the record.
