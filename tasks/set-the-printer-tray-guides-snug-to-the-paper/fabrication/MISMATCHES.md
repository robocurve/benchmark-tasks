# Declared physical/model differences

This is a custom, unbuilt training cassette. Drawings do not establish native simulation or physical performance. A matching runtime must represent the 35 mm plinth, real floor openings, 30 mm cartridges, full pad stroke/stops, cleared bridge, hinge/anchor hardware, compression scales and their moving release/collision surfaces. Existing media is evidence only for its recorded source.

The physical square pawl has 0.5 mm pitch and ±0.05 engagement window; it does not automatically snap into phase. Actual tooth penetration 0.15 and crank lost motion 0.45 give 0.10 rad withdrawal and 0.108 rad for 0.05 clearance. The simulation holds a guide at a tooth only when the released pawl is inside the ±0.05 window or passes a tooth centre; it does not centre an off-phase pawl. Spring/guide holding stiffness and numerical effort caps are not manufactured ratings. The simulation checks the pawl at the 20 Hz control rate; a released carriage that passes a tooth centre between two checks is held at that tooth, as a sprung pawl riding the rack would catch it. This is a sampling proxy, not a measured catch speed.

The physical width link is a free rotating idler with opposed racks. Ideal joint coupling omits backlash/compliance. Split bush drag is dry friction; a 30 N s/m numeric viscous term is not its measured curve. Springs, masses, friction, damping, paper compliance and all acceptance results remain unmeasured. Stock volume and assigned hardware mass estimates are separate from measurements. Rigid 25-sheet simulation bodies are an approximation to four loose 25-sheet groups. Pad compression >2 mm is a benchmark overconstraint proxy, not a proven universal paper bowing onset.

Physical readout uses direct scales with specified uncertainty. Runtime joint displacement can be directly scored but does not prove physical metrology. No dynamics, fatigue, production tooling, assembled CAD collision certification or hardware test is claimed. A built cassette must pass the acceptance tests in CALIBRATION.md before trials; no measured performance of built hardware is claimed.
