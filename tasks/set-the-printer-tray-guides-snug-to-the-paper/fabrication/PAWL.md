# FC1 guided pawl and tie rod

Define T along carriage motion, S across rack width; both zero at rod/pawl centre. Width rod centre U−134→−64, V140; rear U0, V381→231. Fixed width rack extends U−142..−56, V135..145; rear U±5, V201..390. Crest height C is −16 width/−29 rear; roots C−.25, underside C−3.25. The 0.5 mm pitch square crest/valley widths are 0.25/0.25. Pawl teeth width 0.15, depth 0.20; body bottom C+.05 gives 0.15 actual tooth penetration and 0.10 valley-bottom clearance. Eight teeth span 4 mm. The only engagement window is ±0.05 phase per 0.5 pitch. No ramp, nearest-tooth snap or automatic centring exists. Off-phase release is unseated.

Body plan 4×10, total body height 4, top D=C+4.05. Machine main body 3 high and lid 1. Main-body counterbore Ø3.20 is 2 deep from main-body top D−1 to D−3, centred T0, S0. Lid hole Ø2.40; two flush M1.6×3 screws at T0, S±3.5 into 2 mm blind threads. A custom stainless tie rod Ø2 has integral lower pickup button Ø3×1.0. The button is inside the counterbore, not under the teeth. At rest, button top D−1−g, lid underside D−1; gap g=6sin(.10)−.15=0.449, set 0.45 ±0.02. Button bottom D−2.45 leaves 0.55 clearance above counterbore bottom D−3. This is an adjustable measured gap, not assumed from thread turns.

Crank gross lift 6sinθ; net pawl lift=max(0,6sinθ−g). Last tooth withdrawal at θ.10; ≥0.05 tip clearance at asin((g+.20)/6)=0.108 rad. At .17 net lift 0.566. Set actual locked below .09/free by .11 with angle and 0.05 feeler checks. Do not interpret mechanical early loss of a flank due manufacturing error as a commanded release. An off-phase pawl may remain crest-supported; verify depth or apply the hold check after release.

Guide plan bore 4.10×10.10, outer 8.10×16.00; walls 2.0 along T/2.95 along S. Z=C−1..C+8.25 (9.25 high), open underside. The two T-walls cross the fixed rack, so mill them up to Z=C+0.30 over S±5.05 (0.30 above the crests); the S-walls keep Z=C−1 beside the rack. Counterbore and rod are sheltered inside this guide. Cap 2 thick has underside D+4.2, top D+6.2, Ø2.40 rod hole, flat S3 upper seat with no recess. S3 lower seat is lid top D, gap 4.2. S3 OD3.2 is guided by rod Ø2 and guide bore; top seat force 0.15 target. Independent M2 backup stop at T−1, S3.5 from cap above, flat tip D+1.2 with locknut above cap. It limits pawl lift to 1.2 if linkage is mishandled; normal 0.566 lift does not touch it. Two M1.6×5 cap screws at T−2, S±6.5 enter guide walls. Rod/button finish and counterbore Ra≤0.8; no oil on rack teeth.

The guide moves with the carriage, never with the floor. Drop plate N−33.05..−29.05, S±8, bottom C−1, top Z−4. Its upper 90° flange N−34..−24, S±8, Z−4..−2 bolts with two M2×4 at N−27, S±6 into a moving beam Z−2..0. Two M2 screws along N at S±6.5, Z=C+1/C+4 attach guide wall to drop plate; tap 3 mm in POM, finish screw tips flush inside wall. Top flange has rod clearance Ø4 at N−25, S0. Cap top width −5.75 is 1.75 below flange; rear cap is much lower. Gear racks at V130/150 have nearest edges V131/149, guide outer V132..148 gives 1.0 lateral clearance.

Moving width beams: N−30..−20, S±46, Z−2..0; near and far each get one (far has no pawl). Width rail blocks at S±40, Z shaft −11; top plain block −5/drag block −3. Use risers 3/1 respectively to beam underside −2. At each block, mount with two M2 at N−25/−22, local block B1.5 (4.5 toward negative S from shaft), into 3 mm tapped depth. Screws through beam 2+riser 3/1 use M2×8/6. Keep screw bores at B1.5 to avoid the shaft bore, nominal 0.45 ligament. Rear beam N−30..−20, S±66, Z−2..0; rails S±60, Z−25, risers 17 plain/15 drag; M2×22/20. These beams pass above coupling racks with 1 mm gap and carry the drop bracket below. Include all risers as finished custom pieces.

Finished rod is adjusted against the defined eye/pickup assembly rather than a guessed span: at zero eye lower face Z30. Button top is D−1.45 (width Z−13.40; rear Z−26.40). Make rod from one stainless blank with Ø3×1 button, Ø2 shank, top M2 thread 8. Reference length from button top to rod tip: width 46.80, rear 59.80; eye nominal thread engagement 3.401 and jam nut provide ±0.3 mm adjustment. After setting gap, record finished length and lock. Rod tip stays nominal Z33.401, ≤Z33.80, below eye pin bore lower Z34.90. Rod Ø2 through track 4 and beam/cap Ø4/2.4 allows the crank lateral shift; inspect free travel without side-load.

Racks hang from fixed floor standoffs, not from table-mounted heads. Width standoffs Ø4×16.25 at U−139/−59, V140, rear Ø4×29.25 at U0, V205/386. M2 through floor countersunk flush Z3, spacers and top blind 2 mm rack threads: finished screw length 21 width/34 rear gives 1.75 thread engagement, tips never below rack. Rack crest is milled away only under standoff at ends outside pawl sweep. Width pawl envelope U−136..−62 leaves 1 mm to Ø4 standoffs at −139/−59; rear envelope V229..383 leaves 1 mm to rear standoff centre 386. Phase with removable reset gauges and slotted floor holes allowing ±.5 mm, then tighten 0.15 N m and mark. Retest 0/35/70 and 0/75/150 positions. Locked movement ≤0.2 under 10 N is an unperformed acceptance target; the 400 N runtime actuator cap is not a hardware rating.
