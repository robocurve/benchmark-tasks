# Assets and licenses

The iron stand, passive screw and spring clamp, beaker, and thermometer are reused
from the MIT-licensed Read thermometer task. Their dimensions, contact geometry,
materials, spring and screw transmission are retained. The square thermometer
holder is procedural geometry created for this task under the same MIT license.
The original license is retained in `LICENSE`.

DejaVu Sans supplies the printed thermometer scale and beaker markings. Its
license is retained in `assets/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt`. Regenerating these textures
requires `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSans.ttf`; running the supplied
assets does not require that font.

The YAM robot, workstation and calibrated cameras come from yam-arena and are
not copied into this package. OmniGlass and OmniSurface come from Isaac Sim.
No reference photographs or third-party product meshes are distributed.
