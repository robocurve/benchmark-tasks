# Real-world build of the scene

A Benriner BN-1 style flat Japanese mandoline (about 315 x 92 mm, 0.35 to 0.376 kg, 3 mm cut) with its spiked holder
or guard, a board, a bowl and a carrot. The BN-1 lies flat; the scene sits it level on two foot blocks (deck top 5 cm above the board),
so put two 46 mm blocks under its ends. The red handle is its 65 mm end grip, painted or taped red.

- Keep-out box: two yellow lines across the deck, 30 mm either side of the blade line, and a 30 mm tall yellow post (a skewer
  or a card) at each deck edge on those lines.
- Cutting board: beech, 30 x 46 x 2 cm, fixed to the table (it is fixed in the simulation).
- Bowl: clear glass, about 13 cm rim diameter, 4 cm deep, centred under the blade slot; fix it in place.
- Carrot: peel a medium carrot and cut a 3.5 cm stub of 3 cm diameter; stand it on the deck 6 cm behind the blade.
- Food holder: the slicer's own holder, spikes down on the board beside the mandoline's right end.
- Measuring: count slices in the bowl; a front camera for the keep-out box and for hands touching the carrot.
