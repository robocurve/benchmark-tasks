# Real-trial build notes: the form, the trousers, the belt

Everything is an ordinary product. Nothing is scaled, lightened or weakened; where a number in the simulation could
not be sourced it is marked ESTIMATE and the real part's own value should be used.

## The form (what the belt goes round)

- A women's lower-torso display form ("hip form", "butt form"), polyethylene, self-standing: waist 26 in, hips 36 in,
  height 14 in. Example: Fixtures and Displays "Unbreakable Mannequin Collection" milky white ladies lower torso
  form, part PEF-HIPMW (about 55 USD). Any hollow plastic or fibreglass lower-torso form with a 25-27 in waist does.
- It lies on its back on the table with its waist toward the robot, the front of the waist up. Its back rests on the
  table; the top of the waist is then about 20 cm above the table (ESTIMATE: depends on the form's seat). Tape or
  a bead of hot glue under the hips stops it rolling. The waist's near edge is 25 cm from the midpoint between the
  two arm bases.
- The simulation's waist section is an ellipse 24.3 x 17.9 cm (over the jeans), i.e. a 26.7 in circumference over
  the trousers. The form is rigid in the simulation and in the trial.

## The trousers

- Women's jeans in the form's size (US 2-4, 26 in waist), with the five ordinary belt loops (two at the front, one
  at each side-back, one at centre back). The loops must be real sewn denim loops, 36 mm inside width, about 11 mm
  of room for the belt (ESTIMATE; measure the jeans used and state it). The jeans are pulled onto the form and
  the waistband sits at the form's waist; the fly is at the top.
- In the simulation the jeans are a rigid shell over the form (declared in the fidelity note); the five loops are
  rigid bridges of the same room.

## The belt

- A women's 30 mm (1 1/8 in) wide leather belt with a plain single-prong frame buckle, five holes at 1 in (25 mm)
  pitch and a fixed leather keeper. Size: 29 in measured from the fold at the buckle's hinge bar to the middle hole
  (737 mm); most retailers call this a size 30 (measured from the buckle's front edge). Example products of this
  class: Timberland women's 30 mm multi-hole leather belt (self leather keeper); "Traditional Single Leather Belt"
  1 1/8 in with a classic single-prong buckle. Any 30 mm belt with these features and this size will do; a belt
  of the next size up or down shifts which hole is loose by one.
- Strap: leather, 30 mm wide, 3.5 mm thick, about 95 g per metre (ESTIMATE from 900 kg/m^3). The buckle end is
  folded round the hinge bar (two layers) with the keeper stitched 56-69 mm from the bar. Holes are oval,
  5 x 8 mm (ESTIMATE). Hole 1 is nearest the tip; the tip is 150 mm beyond hole 1.
- Buckle: single-prong frame buckle, 4 mm wire or cast bar, inside 33 x 24 mm (across x along the strap), 22 g
  (ESTIMATE). Prong: 3.2 mm diameter, 31 mm from its pivot to its tip, bent up at its root. Reference product:
  a 30 mm single-prong buckle, inner width 30 mm, prong 3.8 mm, length 29 mm (e.g. Pet Hardware ZK0296/30).
- Fastened at its middle hole on this form, the belt is loose by about 6 cm: a belt one size up, worn at its middle
  hole. This is deliberate: a rigid form cannot be drawn in as a waist can, and the spare strap is what lets the
  prong go through a hole at all (see the README).

## Start state (reset in under a minute)

1. Leave the belt through all five loops.
2. Unhook the prong from its hole and let the prong fall back onto the fold under the strap; leave the strap
   through the buckle frame (under the front bar). Pull the tail back until the first hole is 7 cm past the hinge
   bar (layout 0; 7.5 cm and 8 cm for layouts 1 and 2, with the buckle 8 mm to the right or 8 mm to the left of the
   top centre line).
3. Let the tail hang free over the form's left side (the robot's left).
4. Check: no hole on the prong; strap through the frame; belt in all five loops; both hands open and away.

## Wear

Leather holes stretch slightly with use; a belt lasts several hundred fastenings (ESTIMATE) before hole 3 is
noticeably oval. Replace the belt when hole 3 has grown by more than 1 mm in either direction (ruler check).
