# C-A-T Gen 7 trainer product figures, November 2026

Forces measured on a C-A-T Gen 7 trainer with a scale by a technician who works with this product, Nov 2026:

| Quantity | Measured figure | Executable input |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pull to keep winding at lock | 2.2 kg at 65 mm lever | 21.57463 N, 1.40235095 N m |
| Snug-to-lock rotation | 3/4 turn | 270 degrees |
| Push to snap rod in | 0.8 kg | 7.84532 N |
| Pull to lift rod out, measured separately | 0.8 kg | 7.84532 N |
| Rod diameter | 12 mm | 0.012 m |
| Clip mouth | 10 mm | 0.010 m |

Kilogram scale readings are converted using 9.80665 N/kg. Simulation inputs retain the conversion rather than rounding resistance down. The measured torque is approximately 1.40 N m and the two clip peaks approximately 7.8 N.

The record gives the endpoint figures above. The winding spring rises linearly from snug to the measured lock torque, and the model uses that torque as the static recoil at lock. The gauge pressure curve and band tilt stiffness are model choices. No physical robot trial is claimed.
