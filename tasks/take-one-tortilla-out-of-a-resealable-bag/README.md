# take-one-tortilla-out-of-a-resealable-bag

**Instruction:** "Take one tortilla out of the bag and lay it flat on the plate."

A bag of flour tortillas lies flat on a cutting board, its press-to-close zip shut, its mouth toward the arms. The
robot has to open the zip (each lip taken alone and the two pulled apart), get at the top tortilla under the clear
film, slide that one tortilla out without dragging the others, take its edge, and lay it flat on the plate beside
the board. The bag films and the tortillas are simulated as deformable sheets (PhysX surface deformables).

See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for the real-world protocol, the scoring checks and the simulation model.

## Running a trial

From the benchmark-tasks repository root, in the Inspect Robots + yam_arena environment:

```
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy dream \
  -E task=tasks/take-one-tortilla-out-of-a-resealable-bag/task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 \
  -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 \
  --instruction "Take one tortilla out of the bag and lay it flat on the plate." \
  --scorer task_score --max-steps 2400 --store-frames --log-dir ~/yam-eval/tortilla-dream
```

The scripted demo (it plans each phase from the simulation state, then plays joint targets):

```
python tasks/take-one-tortilla-out-of-a-resealable-bag/run_demo.py --out ~/yam-eval/tortilla-demo
```

It writes the logs, `trace.jsonl` (the score's parts every 5 steps), `summary.json` and MP4s in `<out>/videos/`.

- **Score:** `task_score`, judged at the end of the trial (see DESIGN.md); a trial ends early once the full score has
  held for 1 s.
- **Run limit:** 2,400 control steps (120 s at 20 Hz). The demo needs about 1,200 (it ends early once the full score has held for 1 s).
- **Runtime per trial:** about 0.45 s of wall time per control step on an RTX 5090 (about 10 min for the demo).
- **Seeds:** none. The layout is fixed (`object_jitter=0`); GPU PhysX is not bit-for-bit deterministic, so repeated
  demo runs differ slightly.

## Files

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `task.py` | the task file: bodies, sheets, zip, contact rules, score |
| `layout.py` | the geometry: board, plate, bag films, zip and seam pairs, tortillas |
| `clothkit.py` | PhysX surface-deformable sheets for yam_arena task files |
| `zipkit.py` | the zip: rigid rib pairs on breakable joints |
| `gripkit.py` | the pinch and press rules that stand in for rubber-pad friction on sheets |
| `scoring.py`, `test_scoring.py` | the score from the simulation state, and offline tests (`python test_scoring.py`) |
| `plan.py`, `run_demo.py` | the scripted bimanual demo |
| `bodykit.py`, `fastik.py` | multi-shape rigid bodies and fast YAM kinematics (shared helpers) |
| `make_textures.py`, `textures/` | procedural textures (tortillas, the bag's print) |

No third-party assets: every mesh is authored here and every texture is generated by `make_textures.py`.
