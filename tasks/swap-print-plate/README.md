# Swap a finished print plate

A desktop 3D printer stands in front of two YAM arms with a finished print on its plate. A small screen shows the print's status, the bed's temperature falling from 60 to 35 C and a hot-surface symbol until it gets there, and a lamp
shows red, then orange, and turns green at 10 s. The arms must wait for green, carry the used plate level by its two lugs so the loose print stays on, set it on the grey rest, then fit the clean plate from the
blue rest onto the bed. Moving early scores 0, even if the swap then finishes.

## Requirements

- `yam-isaac-lab-arena` with Isaac Lab-Arena 0.3.1 installed (`source <ARENA_DIR>/yam.env`), as on the Brev launchable.
- `printkit.py`, `usdkit.py` and `textures/` are in this folder. They model the printer, plates, rests and print: importing `printkit` hooks `yam_arena.scene` without
  modifying it, so the parts, declared as subclasses of `Box`, spawn from USD files the kit writes at start-up. The hook also runs the lamp's timer.

## Run a trial

From this folder:

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 \
    -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 --policy dream -P rows_per_request=20 --scorer task_score --max-steps 2400 \
    --instruction "The print has finished, but the bed is still hot. Wait until it has cooled: the printer's screen shows the temperature falling and its lamp turns green. Then move the plate with the print onto the grey rest, and then fit the clean plate from the blue rest onto the printer." \
    --store-frames --log-dir out
```

## Scripted demo and failure

`bash run_scripted.sh safe OUT_DIR` runs the demo (score 1.0, about 36 s of simulated time and 730 steps): both arms hold over the bed until 11 s, swaying 2 mm (a still scene makes the renderer hold old pictures on the screen for over a second, and moving wrist cameras clear them), take the used plate by its
lugs, lift it 5 cm, carry it to the grey rest and let go, then do the same with the clean plate from the blue rest to the bed. `bash run_scripted.sh early OUT_DIR` runs the same moves from
2 s, before green, and scores 0 although the swap finishes. The plans are open loop on a fixed clock, so run them with `-E object_jitter=0`.

`inspect-robots video <log>.json` writes one MP4 per camera, and `python make_video.py <frames dir> out.mp4 --setup setup.png --fast-until 11` joins them, writes the setup screenshot and plays the wait at 2x with a 2x label.

## Scoring

`--scorer task_score` prints the total. Per-step `info` is not saved, so the kit also writes the state to `swap_scores/score_*.json` (or `$LIFT_SCORE_DIR`) every 10 control steps and when
the score changes. `printkit.task_score` applies the scoring text in `task.json`, and `_Tracker` latches the failures that do not recover.

## Runtime and seeds

The scene has no random parts with `-E object_jitter=0`, and the seed does not change it. All runs used seed 0.

A Dream-1 trial of 2,400 steps (120 s simulated) took 306 s of wall time on an RTX 5090, with `-P rows_per_request=20` and cameras at 320 x 176. The scripted demo of 726 steps took 99 s at 640 x 360.

The slowest 30 steps took 4.6 s at 320 x 176 with nothing else on the GPU, and 6.72 s during the Dream-1 run. At 20 steps per call, a session stays well under the gateway's limit of about 8 s without a request. `bash run_dream.sh OUT_DIR "<instruction>" 20 2400` runs the trial and stops it if the session is lost.

## Asset sources

- Meshes: our own, generated by `printkit.py` with `usdkit.py`. Sizes follow common 220 mm desktop bed-slinger printers: a 20 x 40 mm extrusion frame, 42 mm stepper motors, 8 mm rods and lead screws, 8 mm locating pins. The plate and bed are
  smaller, to fit the arms' reach.
- Textures, from ambientCG (CC0): Metal032 (`steel_*`), Plastic012A (`plastic_*`), Plastic008 (`blue_paint`). Our own, generated by `make_textures.py`: `pei_*`, `pla_*`, and `screen_atlas.png`, the screen's pictures, drawn in DejaVu Sans Bold (free licence).
- Arms, table, lights and cameras: the harness's station, unchanged.

## Changes from the first design

- Bed centred at 41 cm rather than 40 cm, and the rests side by side at 17.5 cm, 13.5 cm either side of the middle: beside each other as first drawn, the plates' inner lugs overlapped.
- Rests 23 x 22 cm rather than 20 x 20 cm, so their colours show round a plate at 320 x 176.
- Plates of aluminium with a PEI surface rather than steel, which at this size would weigh 1 kg rather than 0.6 kg.
- Pins: two behind the plate and one at its left. The scorer also tests the plate's centre, since three pins do not close all four sides.
- On review: the bed cools in 10 s rather than 20 s; a screen shows temperature, status and a hot-surface symbol; the lamp shows red, orange and green; and the instruction says the wait is needed.
- Early move is any move over 1 cm, not only a rise, so sliding the plate off does not avoid it. The scorer also enforces the order "then" in the instruction.
