# Reference and asset sources

| Source | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| [Tektronix TDS1000B/2000B User Manual, pp. 7 and 107–115](https://download.tek.com/manual/071181702web.pdf) | Manual probe compensation on CH1, 10× on probe and scope, dedicated reference/output return, and its nominal 5 V at 1 kHz. The nominal DC input is 1 MΩ in parallel with 20 pF. The instrument weighs about 2.0 kg and has a 145 mm diagonal 320 × 240 LCD. The 4:3 active panel is represented at approximately 116 × 87 mm. |
| [Tektronix TDS2000B datasheet](https://www.tek.com/en/datasheet/digital-storage-oscilloscope-0) | TDS2024B case size, 326.3 mm wide × 158.0 mm high × 124.2 mm deep. |
| [Tektronix P2220 instructions, pp. 2–5](https://download.tek.com/manual/071146400.pdf) and [compatibility FAQ](https://www.tek.com/en/support/faqs/what-difference-between-p2220-and-p2221) | The P2220 has a 1.5 m cable and supports scope inputs from 15 to 25 pF. The nominal 20 pF input of the TDS2024B is in that range. The P2220 presents 13–17 pF at its tip in 10× mode. The documented 003-1433 tool accesses the trimmer radially through an opening near the BNC. The manual compares the rounded, peaked and compensated waveforms. |
| [Tektronix adjustment equipment list](https://download.tek.com/manual/071038201web_6.pdf), [tool description](https://download.tek.com/manual/P6021A-Current-Probe-Instruction-Manual.pdf), and [replaceable parts list](https://download.tek.com/manual/070969501.pdf) | The same 003-1433-00 is listed as an example of an adjustment tool with a nonconductive shaft ≤2.5 mm in diameter. It has an insulated flat blade with a metal tip and black plastic body. |
| [Digilent compensated probe explanation](https://digilent.com/blog/why-do-i-need-to-do-bnc-probe-compensation/) | Divider capacitance and scope input affect transient response. The code uses a standard two-branch RC divider with 9 MΩ series and 1 MΩ termination. |

The modeled trimmer port width and position, blade and grip dimensions, tool mass, contact parameters and turning resistance are prototype estimates. The 2.5 mm shaft follows the manufacturer equipment requirement. The circuit's effective capacitance includes an estimated cable contribution. The supported scope input range establishes pairing compatibility.

Task assets are original primitives and a generated waveform mesh. Manuals and product illustrations are reference links. I2RT YAM assets come from the pinned dependency with the MIT notice named in the [README](README.md). The task records with its calibrated station, without adding another camera.
