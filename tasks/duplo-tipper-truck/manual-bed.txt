Finish the tipper truck, then test it by dumping the orange cube into the grey tray. The truck stands on the table with its cab, blue brick and black hinge plate already built on; the blue tipper bed lies loose. The front of the truck is the end toward the right arm; the rear, with the hinge pin, faces the tray. Hold the truck still while you press so it does not roll. 1. Hook the clip at the open end of the blue tipper bed onto the hinge pin, open end toward the rear and the bed tilted up a little, let the bed swing down onto the truck, then press the clip down over the pin until it snaps on. The bed now rests closed and can tip back on its hinge. 2. Put the orange cube in the bed. 3. Tip the bed back on its hinge until the cube slides out through the open rear into the tray, then let go of the bed. 4. Finish with the truck upright on its wheels and both grippers open and away from it.
