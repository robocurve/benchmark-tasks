Build the tipper truck on the white car base, then test it by dumping the orange cube into the grey tray. The front of the truck is the end toward the right arm; the rear, with the tow pin, faces the tray. Press each part straight down until it snaps on, and hold the truck still while you press so it does not roll. 1. Put the blue 2x2 brick on the base, centred across its width, with its centre 40 mm in front of the middle of the base. 2. Put the yellow cab over the base and the brick: cabin at the front, its rear deck flush with the rear of the base. Press it down. 3. Put the black hinge plate on the cab's rear deck, centred across the width, with its studded square right behind the cabin and its hinge pin at the rear. Press it down. 4. Hook the clip at the open end of the blue tipper bed onto the hinge pin, open end toward the rear and the bed tilted up a little, let the bed swing down onto the truck, then press the clip down over the pin until it snaps on. The bed now rests closed and can tip back on its hinge. 5. Put the orange cube in the bed. Tip the bed back on its hinge until the cube slides out through the open rear into the tray, then let go of the bed. 6. Finish with the truck upright on its wheels and both grippers open and away from it.
