# Accessible comparisons and limits

Comparison scope: the batch's frozen2260-row catalog,2258PRs through2259,608 official board definitions, the verified earlier full-reading baseline plus assigned material deltas, and1728 public formal benchmark definitions. Detailed raw retrieval is outside the portable task in the batch's `worker3-reading/anti-comparisons.json`. No claim is made about private benchmarks, official ownership expiry or acceptance.

- PR813/TK-VVBP87, TK-ZEREPP, **Set a carriage preload for rigidity and low drag**: blue setting tab/orange lock changes a V-wheel carriage's equivalent stiffness and drag; prove lateral rigidity and axial travel. This is the closest generic preload-adjustment ability. Here the object is an already-threaded commercial two-part lead nut: a stainless sleeve's contacting ramps and torsion spring unload/reload opposed screw flanks. It has no preload tab/lock or carriage travel proof. Whether this difference is substantive belongs to independent review.
- PR1110/TK-ZSQRU3, **Calibrate a torque limiting clutch against two proof loads**: set a preload slider then show transmission/slip at two external loads. This task changes neither a clutch capacity control nor load selector, and does not calibrate two proof loads. Both deal with friction/preload; that shared relationship alone does not resolve novelty.
- PR986, **Wind a torsion spring three quarter turns, passing the cone from hand to hand, and pin it**: fixed hub/bars/bridges and a latching pin capture winding. The current cam has no handover obstacle or latching pin and is allowed to unwind into contact preload, not locked against a stored-angle target.
- PR779, **Calibrate two backlash dials to their target setpoints and lock them**: dial/LED deadband and lock buttons, not physical removal of opposed thread-flank clearance by an elastic cam.
- PR347, **Set shaft endplay using a split spacer**: infer clearance from stops and choose a spacer; the current task uses continuous spring-loaded ramp contact and no spacer choice.
- PR70, **Aim a geared pointer whose gears have play**: identify a hidden button-step/backlash model and set an angle/report card. Here there are no button-driven states or reported inference result.
- PR1733, **Capture a twelve-cell pack's shared spring preload with a cross-key**: force-sharing under a pressure plate and inserting a retaining key, not a lead-nut cam reset.
- PR1711, **Take a spring-loaded bayonet coupling sleeve off its shaft**: a different push/turn/lift/reverse track-release manipulation, ending with loose-part placement. This task keeps the nut mounted and corrects a passive preload state.
- PR1898/TK-JUWHH6, **Assemble a rack-and-pinion parallel gripper so both fingers open evenly**: contact mesh/tooth phasing, not cam-friction hysteresis and axial thread-flank bias.
- PR2203/TK-73Y6MQ, **Set timing-belt tension and secure a robot joint's motor mount**: adjust pulley centre distance, bolt-clamp the plate, read belt force/deflection. This task retains a torsion-biased screw nut with no belt, bolt tightening or motor-plate tension setting.
- PR2229/TK-XJ2DTF, **Set a proximity sensor's gap with a feeler and lock its cam clamp**: translate the sensor to a dog, clamp on a leaf, remove the leaf. A clamping lever/feeler is different from a cam sleeve that directly spreads the nut halves on a screw; generic precision setting is shared.

This team's earlier **ballnut-sleeve-transfer** is a mandatory own-family comparison. It transfers a full-size ballnut and50 uncaged balls across a tightly aligned support-pipe/screw butt joint while preserving the raceway, then turns the nut along a real lead. The current solid threaded split nut has no balls, shipping sleeve, butt alignment or nut transfer. Conversely this candidate's stationary thread joint cannot perform the ballnut task. Both belong to screw-actuator building, so that family relationship is explicitly retained for review.

Public-pool searches for anti-backlash/backlash/lead screw/split nut/torsion/preload found no exact formal definition. The closest retrieved screw operation, RoboDojo **Fasten Screws (Simulation)**, inserts and tightens colour-matched screws into nuts; it does not correct a spring-cam bias on an already-engaged two-part nut. Generic tool-packing/gear-cleaning definitions surfaced by broad words are not asserted as equivalent physical tasks. Public definitions were read; no external simulator was executed and no inaccessible/private retrieval was claimed.
