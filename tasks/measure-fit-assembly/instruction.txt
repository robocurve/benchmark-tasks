Measure, fit and verify the shaft assembly using this procedure. 1. Lift the handheld contact indicator and place both reference pads against the housing's rear datum face. Keep its spring probe aligned with the shaft end while moving the shaft gently to both axial stops. The remote display is in millimetres; subtract the lower reading from the higher reading to obtain axial travel. Hold the instrument steady at each stop before reading. 2. Select a split spacer using this tolerance table: measured travel 8–10 mm → blue 6 mm spacer; 11–13 mm → yellow 9 mm spacer; 16–18 mm → red 14 mm spacer. If the result falls outside these ranges, recheck it. The required final axial travel is 2–4 mm. 3. Move the shaft to its near stop, withdraw the indicator axially and return it to the table. Insert the selected split spacer from above into the housing channel, with its opening downward and its grasp tab upward. Leave the fixed end stop in place. Do not force an oversized spacer into the channel. 4. Repeat the referenced measurement at both shaft stops. Verify 2–4 mm remaining travel. With the spacer retained, turn the shaft through at least 60 degrees to verify that the fit permits rotation. 5. Return the indicator to the table, release the assembly and withdraw both hands. The fitted spacer must remain seated and the assembly stable for two seconds.
