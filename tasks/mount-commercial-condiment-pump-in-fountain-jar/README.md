# Mount Commercial Condiment Pump in Fountain Jar

## Overview
In commercial fast food and high-volume food service operations (BLS SOC 35-3023: Fast Food and Counter Workers, 3,460,000 workers; BLS SOC 35-2021: Food Preparation Workers, 891,000 workers; total >4.35 million US workers), condiment dispensing stations are disassembled, sanitized, and reassembled daily under FDA Food Code standards. Stainless portion-control dispenser pumps (Server Products / Vollrath commercial standard) are mounted and secured into heavy-gauge stainless steel fountain jars.

## Mechanics & Physics
- **Stainless Steel Fountain Jar** (110 x 130 x 75 mm, 1.2 kg) sits on the prep counter at `(0.260, 0.080)` m with a flared rim (`Z = 0.8225` m), welded side loop handle at `(0.260, -0.015, 0.7865)` m, and front locking detent catch.
- **Sanitary Wire Wash Rack** (140 x 140 x 65 mm, 0.50 kg) at `(0.240, 0.250)` m with a 50 mm deep vertical socket holding the sanitized pump upright.
- **Commercial Portion-Control Pump** (380 g) with a 65 mm pick-up tube, 118 x 138 mm rectangular contoured lid collar, spring-loaded locking detent tab, and vertical grip stem.
- **Bimanual Coordination**: Right gripper grasps the jar's side loop handle to steady against insertion forces and torques. Left gripper retrieves the pump from the wash rack socket, coaxially guides the pick-up cylinder into the jar bore without jamming, seats the collar flush onto the flared rim, pushes the spring locking collar tab forward into the rim detent slot, and applies an upward proof pull against the mechanical detent to verify retention before clearing both hands for a 1.0 s stability dwell.

## Stages & Scoring
- **0.20**: Pump grasped and lifted vertically clear of wash rack socket (> 30 mm).
- **0.40**: Pick-up cylinder entered jar bore (`r_xy < 0.035` m, vertical tip insertion `tube_z < rim_z - 0.010` m).
- **0.65**: Lid collar seated flush on jar rim (`r_xy < 0.025` m, vertical gap < 15 mm).
- **0.85**: Collar locking tab pushed forward into rim detent slot (`c_x - j_x < -0.002` m).
- **1.00**: Upward proof pull sustained against mechanical detent for >= 10 steps (0.5 s), both hands retracted clear (TCPs >= 35 mm, link hulls >= 5 mm clear), and assembly resting still.
- **Defect latches (0.0)**: Dropped pump outside stand/jar, excessive jar displacement (> 35 mm) or tipping, or hands not clear during hold.
