# Outcome rubric

For the final 1 s, the stencil must rest released and still on its supports. The blade and handle may park beside the PCB at any height; any portion over it must clear its top by >2 mm. A bridge or interarm collision gives 0. A pad qualifies at ≥75% coverage. Score 1 for ≥90% qualifying, ≥50% on every pad, ≥80% of deposit area on pads and ≥50 mg on the PCB; 0.5 for ≥50% qualifying and ≥25 mg on the PCB; 0.25 for ≥25% and ≥12.5 mg on the PCB; otherwise 0.

The ending state is graded; a previous success does not survive a subsequent disturbance. No camera movement, arm allocation, named grasp, approach angle or scripted waypoint is scored. Native support, release and low motion must hold continuously for one second. Translational speed limit2mm/s and angular speed0.1rad/s are the numerical stillness convention. Nonfinite state and invalid contacts fail closed.

Material-response constants and limitations are in MATERIAL_MODEL.md. A final-frame photo and the last second of video document release/support and visible finish. See PHYSICAL_GRADING.md for real-trial checks. Native fullscore and copied-source replay verification passed for the supplied demo.

Any connected paste bridge disqualifies all score bands. The 50% minimum per-pad coverage and 80% on-pad area apply to full credit; partial bands use their stated qualified-pad and total-mass thresholds.

For the entire final one-second hold, the returned frame rests on at least one frame support, is released and still, and its centre in plan is more than 80 mm from the PCB centre. For squeegee clearance use its complete steel blade and handle in their current orientation. The checked volume has the PCB footprint (68.58 × 53.34 mm), extending from the board underside to 2 mm above the bare top face. Touching this volume interrupts the hold. A tool parked wholly beside the board passes at any height. Where the tool projects over the board, that portion can clear it vertically. The printed-board checks apply at every score band.
