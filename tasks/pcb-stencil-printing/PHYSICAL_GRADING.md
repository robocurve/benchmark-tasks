# Physical grading


Before the trial, weigh the clean, dry PCB on an ordinary1mg-resolution balance.
After printing and clearing the board, weigh it again. Full credit requires at
least50mg deposited; the partial bands require25mg and12.5mg respectively. Take
a close-up photo of the entire board, with enough resolution to resolve each
pad. Use the included pad-layout drawing as the overlay: grade the area covered
by visible paste and inspect every inter-pad gap for a connected paste bridge.
The numeric coverage is the union of deposited footprints, so overlapping paste
is not counted twice and material from any aperture may cover any target pad.
The simulation omits deposits thinner than10µm from both visible geometry and
coverage; its nominal density is4.2g/mL. This is a disclosed visible-deposit
approximation, not an inaccessible per-pad thickness test or IPC qualification.
Return/release and board clearance are checked from the final second of video.

Use a disposable bench liner around the fixtures and keep the gripping surfaces
dry. Inspect consumables before each trial, use the same batch and room-temperature
preload, and clean/replenish them between runs. The trial uses no heating or live
biological sample. Keep solder paste on the PCB/stencil/tool and follow its product handling instructions.
