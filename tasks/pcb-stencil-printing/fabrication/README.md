# Fixture geometry

STL units are millimetres. These are passive bench supports; dimensions and
origins in geometry.py define the assembled fixed layout. Print or machine the
blocks and secure them to the bench. They add no handles, active joints, hidden
locks or post-reset motion. All workpieces and tools remain separate at reset.
These files are engineering drawings, not a claim of a physical fit test.

For real trials use disposable thin finger-pad protection and a removable bench
liner; keep liquid/paste off the gripper pads by grasping the dry edges, frame or
handle. Replace consumable liquid, coverslip or gel and clean the implements for
every trial. Do not use living cultures in the repeatable stand-in protocol.

The steel foil is125µm thick. Frame bars surround its sharp outer edge. Use the
1:1SVG and included board source for a laser-cut stencil. Four skirt sections
are attached below the frame/foil datum as shown in geometry.py; their nominal
PCB clearance is50µm per side. Measure the actual board outline and fit-check
this pocket before a real trial; board-fabrication tolerances are not reproduced
by the nominal model. No physical fit or commercial stencil qualification is
claimed. The Adafruit source licence and attribution remain in reference/.
