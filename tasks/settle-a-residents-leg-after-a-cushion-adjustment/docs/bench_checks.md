# Grade a recorded mannequin trial

One reader fills 21 entries for the full trial. Keep the two synchronized videos, heel-channel calibration and station dimensions. Number the **scored** samples 1–280 at 20 Hz, starting after the initial camera image. The reset occupies frames 1–5. The terminal dwell occupies frames 240–280 inclusive. A second reader can use a separate copy and compare the result.

Copy [the interval worksheet](bench_checks.csv). For each check, enter the observed value and an evidence locator such as a recording filename, view and frame number or range. Cite the limiting frame or frames for extrema. Enter absolute measurement uncertainty in the same units for continuous readings, including uncertainty from image scale and perspective. Leave the uncertainty column empty for discrete counts, event windows and coverage. Enter `?` for an unreadable or ambiguous observation. From the task folder run:

```bash
python3 -m tools.bench path/to/completed-checks.csv
```

The interval sheet uses the Python standard library. A readable trial yields the score and reason. Missing data, unresolvable uncertainty on a required decision, and invalid resets exit with status 2. Zero uncertainty is appropriate for exact digital replay. Use the measured resolution and calibration errors for bench observations. The [detailed worksheet](bench_worksheet.csv) remains available for measurement-by-measurement reconstruction.

| Interval | Readout and entries |
|---|---|
| Entire trial | Verify 280 consecutive synchronized, readable observations and record `1-280` for coverage. Playback can be paused or slowed. Check the whole episode for both fault events. |
| Reset | Read the five force values in newtons once each. The software takes their median. Enter the largest heel-to-nearest-support gap across these frames in mm, and the greatest number of calf regions bearing on foam in any single reset frame. A fully loaded reset here means the median heel load is at least 5 N. |
| Fault events | In `opposing_grip_windows`, enter the frame ranges with both fingers of one gripper on opposing sides of the same mannequin segment. In `extra_heel_load_with_arm_contact_windows`, enter ranges when **both** a robot link is within 1 mm of the mannequin **and** heel load exceeds 1.5 times the reset median. For each event write all maximal runs, e.g. `30-32 51-51 96-107`, or `none` after reviewing the video. The first 11 consecutive observations of either event latch zero. Separated events cannot be combined. If calibration uncertainty affects whether an event was present, enter `?`; include uncertainty in both the baseline and the instantaneous load. |
| Dwell motion and sling | Over all 41 observations, measure peak-to-peak displacement **for each individual marker on each individual ruler axis**. Track the heel, knee and four cushion-base corners. Enter the largest of the resulting 18 excursions. A marker that moves out and back counts. Enter `1` for slack only when the sling is unloaded throughout, otherwise `0`. |
| Dwell load, orientation and gaps | Enter the largest heel reading in N. Enter the largest cushion-base to bed gap and the smallest vertical component of the upward cushion-base normal (1 for level, or cosine of measured tilt). Enter three separate minima in mm: clearance from cushion to Achilles/back of knee, clearance from **any** robot link to the mannequin, and heel-to-nearest-support clearance including the cushion and bed. The parked-clearance field is the smallest distance from either distal arm to the mannequin or cushion. Measure each extreme over the whole dwell. |
| Dwell calf bearing | On each frame, a stripe bears where its depression is at least 3 mm relative to its own tare and it lies within 2 mm of the marked calf region. Count how many of the two regions are bearing **in that frame**. Enter the smallest count seen, 0, 1 or 2. For each frame, compute the center spacing of its outermost bearing stripes, using 43 mm per step and 0 for fewer than two stripes. Enter the smallest of those spans. Two regions that bear only at different times do not provide simultaneous two-region support. |

Use calibrated masks or movable reference lines to locate threshold crossings on playback. If a marker is hidden, a threshold cannot be resolved in both planes or a view is missing, the reader cannot certify coverage. Keep the actual images and analog record alongside the sheet. This short form reduces transcription. Demonstrating repeatable physical measurements still requires a built fixture and independent readers.
