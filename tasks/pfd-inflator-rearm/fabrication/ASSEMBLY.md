# Build the unpressurised training bench

Use the six millimetre STL masters in this folder. They form one matched dry training fixture. The production references are the Mustang MD3075 manual head and MA7203 cartridge interface. Install only the solid training pieces in this bench. The original equipment manuals describe the separate inspection of the metal face and adapter ring, the lever, the turn and the final indication. They do not specify our internal fork or the indicator response to our deliberately mismatched training part. [MD3075, printed pp. 6–7](https://www.machovec.com/mustang/documentation/manuals/md3075_10aug2011.pdf#page=8) · [MA7203 figures](https://www.machovec.com/mustang/documentation/manuals/ma7203_05oct2011.pdf)

## Parts for one bench

| Quantity | Part | Preparation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 each | `receiver.stl`, `rocker.stl`, `shutter.stl` | Print rigid PETG at 1:1, or use a dimensionally equivalent rigid process. The receiver includes the base, bayonet banks, stops, viewing ports, hinge upright and slide guide. The two moving masters are separate components. |
| 1 each | `original.stl`, `intact.stl`, `reject.stl` | Print at full density. All three pieces are solid, vented training dummies with a blind ballast socket. Use the same metallic paint on each body, the same dark paint on the adapter and O-ring profiles, and the same pale lock stripes. The original lacks the outer shoulder ring. |
| 1 set | Ø3 mm smooth hinge pin or shoulder fastener, 25–30 mm usable length, 3 mm spacer, washers and retainers | The 10 mm moving hub and 8 mm fixed ear have Ø3.3 mm bores on the common Y axis. Use spacers and a retainer appropriate to the chosen pin, leaving small axial float. |
| 2 sets | Ø2 mm smooth shank pins, washers and retainers | One 12–16 mm pin crosses the shutter guide and the vertical slot in the tongue. One 14–18 mm pin attaches to the rocker tail, spans the curved stop slot and provides a spring attachment under its head. Keep threads out of sliding interfaces. |
| 1 | Light extension spring with hooks and a short wire link if needed | Choose for roughly 9–14 N at the working eye separation of approximately 26–28 mm. The two anchors are on the outboard tail and the fixed eye above it. Final selection is by paddle effort and return, below. |
| As needed | Ø8 mm steel rod for the three dummies, Ø9 mm steel rod for the rocker, M3 washers and short fasteners for the shutter | Trim the rods to give 102 g per dummy and 22 g for the complete moving rocker (including its pin on the tail). Weigh and secure the assembled shutter including its top washers at 6 g. The dummy ballast bore is Ø8.4 × 92 mm. The rocker's open ballast sleeve is Ø9.2 × 36 mm. Use a denser solid slug if a coupon print is too light for steel; keep the rod ends inside the envelope and bond them. |
| 1 set | Four M4 hold-downs, red/green/yellow/pale paint, two removable opaque paper masks, ordinary cups or dividers, two stock pads, orange cloth and grey webbing | Match the pad, bin and textile dimensions and positions in the workcell drawing. Provide a 0.1 g scale, a ruler, and a spring scale for initial setup. A phone camera can record the final handoff. |

The receiver fits inside 126 × 146 × 231 mm. Allow additional printer height for bed adhesion. Separate, orient and support each STL appropriately; their origins preserve assembly coordinates. Remove all support material from the shelves, bores, slots and fork. Dry-fit and deburr the hinge and smooth the guide until the shutter falls freely under its own weight. Prepare matching ballast and paint before the trials. A planning allowance of USD 40–85 covers filament, metal stock, spring, fasteners, marking materials and bins at small quantities if a printer and ordinary bench tools are already available. This is a materials estimate.

## Assembly

1. Bolt down the receiver on the dry work surface. Its local origin is the socket axis at table height. With the stock pads in front, positive Y points towards the long rocker support. Plate hole centres in mm are (−48, 8), (−48, 101), (58, −24) and (58, 101). Lay the orange panel and webbing around the base, clear of the moving parts and tray floors. [Bench coordinates](../WORKCELL.md#fixed-bench)
2. With the paddle level, introduce the rocker from the negative X side. Align the hub with the fixed ear, insert the hinge pin along Y and retain it. The 3 mm spacer occupies the gap between hub and ear. At the short tail fit the Ø2 mm shank through its curved slot and retain it with clearance. Hook the spring from the opposite end of the tail pin to the fixed eye above. The paddle should rise freely to its upper stop, approximately 32°, and return to level. Check effort perpendicular to the paddle about 53 mm from the pivot. A reference simulation measured approximately 2.9 N of contact force in the held position during a turn. Aim for approximately 2–4 N with the paddle raised 15–16°; this is a setup target for the training replica.
3. Lower the shutter vertically from above. The two long probes enter the opposed outer slots, the flag slides behind the bezel and the rear tongue enters the open-sided guide. Pass the guide pin along X through the tongue slot and retain it. Full axial travel is about 12 mm. Colour the lower 3.7 mm band of the flag green and the upper 3.6 mm band red. The unloaded shutter should fall to red when released. [Assembly drawing](assembly-guide.pdf)
4. Finish the three metal faces before marking the clock position. The underside ballast socket is distinct from the small inspection recess at the opposite end. On a correctly seated training dummy mark both lock indices as shown in the workcell. Apply identical stripes to all three parts. The two 5.5 mm wide inspection ports in the printed receiver admit a side view at the opposed lock positions. Cover them with opaque paper masks during a robot episode. For judging, remove the masks from the fixed housing after recording the hands-free interval, with the bottle undisturbed.

## Commissioning and reset

Perform these checks with the dry dummies before scoring. Record the observed results. If either spare binds, or if their window colours differ when both are correctly seated, adjust the hardware before using the bench.

| Set by hand | Expected on this dry bench | What the check establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Empty head, first at rest and then holding the rocker raised | Red in both cases | A lever movement alone cannot operate the flag. |
| Original in the locked position, hands clear | Red, body suspended | Reference initial state with a missing outer ring. |
| Intact spare and then reject spare fully seated, tested separately | Both hang from both lugs. Both show green. The two metal faces remain visibly different before installation. | Each matched inert spare can fit and drive the same outer-ring indicator. A green reading cannot identify the correct spare. |
| Intact dummy below the ledges with marks aligned; release over a padded catch during commissioning | The marks alone do not retain it | Both lugs must actually engage. Remove the catch before an evaluation. |
| Original collected, reject in red tray, intact spare on pad, empty head | Red; all three parts accounted for | Repeatable isolated handoff. |

For each trial, return the original to its locked red starting position; empty the grey cup and red tray. Set A then B as specified in the workcell. Check both views of the fixed socket before replacing the grading masks. Use only the policy's three calibrated views during a robot episode. In a physical handoff, both lugs should be over their shelves, both grippers clear and the bottle clear of auxiliary support. A side touch at the rotation stop is compatible with a suspended bottle. Use both ports if its support is ambiguous. If the relevant view was missed, mark that handoff ungradable and recover a view before disturbing it.

The digital checks cover assembly access, separate moving parts, the nominal motion range, both dry adapters' insert/turn/seat path and visibility through the grading ports. Print tolerances, friction, spring selection and repeated commissioning on a built fixture require a physical check. This fixture exercises the exposed dry service step and supplies no pressure, leak or return-to-wear certification.

The STL files are ready to slice in millimetres. To regenerate them from the reference geometry, run `python fabrication/build.py` from the task folder in a separate CAD environment with NumPy, trimesh and manifold3d. The simulator itself does not import the fabrication generator.
