# Open a tacked seam without damaging the fabric

Use the complete video, a close photograph and a millimetre ruler. The six red
stitches are individually visible in a row. Count a stitch as open only when
its cut ends have a gap of at least 2 mm. Merely touching it with the ripper, or
a software cut event without visible separation, does not count. Loose cut
thread may remain in the original needle holes; clearing those remnants is
not part of this task.

- 0: fewer than two open stitches.
- 0.25: two or three open stitches.
- 0.5: four or five open stitches.
- 0.75: all six open stitches.
- 1: all six open, with the ripper resting on the table at least 10 mm from both
  patches and both robot hands released for the final 3 seconds.

Any blade cut through canvas, fabric tear, or needle hole enlarged by more
than 2 mm beyond its original boundary makes the score zero. Pulling the patches
apart by breaking the fabric does not earn partial credit. Judge the final
state; a stitch that remains connected does not count.

The simulator records actual thread endpoints, tool and cloth geometry. Its
native panel-fracture and blade-crossing checks represent the damage rule.
Small numerical contact residuals are not textile tears. This is an approximate
material model, not a calibrated predictor of textile cutting forces.

The included evidence comes from complete fresh-start demo and Dream trials.
Development checkpoints are excluded.
