1. Take hold of the white pump barrel with one gripper and the yellow handle with the other, and lift the pump a few centimetres off the grey mat together. 2. Keep the barrel still and pump: push the handle in toward the barrel, then pull it fully out again; repeat. 3. While pumping, watch the dial on the grey gauge housing (to the right of the pump, farther from the robots): its black needle turns clockwise as the pressure rises. The target is the red 60 kPa mark, which points straight away from the robots, in the middle of the green band (54 to 66 kPa). Stop pushing the handle immediately when the needle reaches the red mark, even partway through a stroke. Never exceed 80 kPa. 4. Put the pump back down on the grey mat with both of its feet on the mat, then open both grippers and move them away. 5. Check the dial again: the needle must still be within the green band.
