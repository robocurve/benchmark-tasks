1. Verify the isolated two-wire harness. On the upright panel, A is lower left and B upper left; C lower right and D upper right. Pick up the orange and blue probes. 2. Measure AC, AD, BC and BD. Hold both spring tips on the named pads for at least 0.5 s. The meter shows 1 for a connected 1-ohm path, OL for no path, and -- when a tip is disconnected. OL counts only while both tips visibly contact the pads. Withdraw before changing pairs. 3. Write the four results in AC,AD,BC,BD order, using 1 for continuity and 0 for OL: 1001 means PASS, 0110 means CROSSED, 1000 or 0001 means OPEN. Any other pattern needs rechecking. 4. Return both probes flat to their starting places. Move the blue result tag onto P for PASS, X for CROSSED or O for OPEN. Release it, open both grippers and withdraw. Leave the probes and tag still for two seconds.
