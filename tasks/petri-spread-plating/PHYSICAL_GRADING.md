# Physical grading


Use a standard pre-poured90mm nominal agar plate at20–25°C and100µL nonliving
blue-dyed water as the repeatable stand-in; do not culture microorganisms for
this benchmark. Use a fresh plate and clean tool for each trial. Photograph the
open plate immediately after the final closed-state check, with a scale. Overlay
an80mm circle and four quadrants aligned with the four cardinal rim ticks; grade visibly spread dye coverage, not tool
path length or a prescribed streak pattern. A1mm grid supports the stated area
percentages. Inspect for visible gouges with raking light and a1mm reference
square. The simulator's0.3mm indentation witness represents such damage; it is
not a claim of measured biological viability or calibrated agar tearing.
Video must show the lid returned, the tool on its rack and the final release.

Use a removable bench liner and keep all jaw contact surfaces dry. The
nonliving tracer and fresh pre-poured plate are consumables; no incubation,
flame sterilization or biological viability result is part of this benchmark.

Before a real trial, add four small ink ticks at the ends of two perpendicular
diameters on the dry base rim. The scoring grid is aligned to these marks,
so dish rotation does not make the quadrant reference unobservable.
