1. The puller is already prepared: both hooks sit behind the orange collar on the fixed shaft. Grasp the grey end block on its side handle to keep the loose tool aligned. 2. With the other gripper, turn the orange screw knob clockwise, regrasping as needed, until the collar clears the shaft; keep supporting the tool. 3. Use both grippers to lift the loaded tool clear and place its crossbar upright on the two grey rest posts over the green tray. 4. Spread the two sliding jaws and let the collar settle fully inside the tray. Open both grippers and withdraw. The collar must stay in the tray and the tool on its rest for 2 seconds.
