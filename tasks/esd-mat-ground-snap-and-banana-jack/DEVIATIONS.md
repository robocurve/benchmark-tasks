# Mechanical & Simulation Deviations — `esd-mat-ground-snap-and-banana-jack`

- **Mass floor scaling (`>= 26 g` vs real `< 15 g` molded snap/wire segments)**: Real `10 mm` brass/PVC snap caps, `4 mm` banana plugs, and cantilevered detent clips weigh `6-18 g`; in simulation every dynamic body is scaled to `26-50 g` (`SNAP_CAP_MASS = 0.034 kg`, `PLUG_MASS = 0.034 kg`, `BOOT_MASS = 0.026 kg`, `CORD_LINK_MASS = 0.030 kg`, `JAW_MASS = 0.028 kg`, `TAB_MASS = 0.050 kg`, `GB_MASS = 0.120 kg`) to ensure clean PhysX solver mass conditioning against robot gripper links.
- **Discretized coiled cord & boots**: The `1.8 m` helical coiled wire is modelled as a 6-body articulated chain (`snap_cap` -> pitch `strain_boot` -> yaw `cord_prox` -> yaw `cord_dist` -> yaw `plug_boot` -> pitch `banana_plug`) spanning a fixed `318 mm` bench footprint rather than a continuum viscoelastic helix.
- **Bimanual corner retainer (`tab_catch`) & beveled catch sliders**: Elastic flap curl is modelled via a `0.35 N*m/rad` revolute hinge (`rest_deg = +14.0`) paired with a separate `28 g` `tab_catch` (`14 N/m` return) on the fixed bench; the `10 mm` snap ring and banana-plug spring cage are represented as separate `28 g` `60 deg` beveled horizontal sliders (`snap_jaw` at `22 N/m` and `jack_latch` at `6 N/m`) rather than compliant axisymmetric sheet-metal rings (`SelfCollisions=False` safe).
- **Gripper pad-caging flanges on `banana_plug`**: Added `-Y`/`+Y` caging shoulders (`24-26 mm` wide, `4 mm` thick) above the pad contact zone (`z = 28-33 mm`) to prevent unphysical longitudinal finger pad slip under low friction (`0.7x-0.8x`) during `-Y` insertion against `jack_latch`.
