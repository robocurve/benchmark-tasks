1. Pinch the blue release control against the grey handle and keep it squeezed. With the other gripper, take the orange hook head by its dark neck. 2. Point the hook sideways, align its plate slots with the pin lugs and lower it fully. Turn the head a quarter turn to the stop, hook pointing along the shaft away from the handle. 3. Let the release return, then grasp the fixed rear handle. Lift the tool and check that the head stays attached. 4. Slide the hook tooth-down into the grey hood. Lower the tooth into the pocket behind the blue cartridge's front lip and pull until the cartridge is fully outside. 5. Lift the hook out and place the cartridge fully inside the green tray. Return the intact tool to its start, flat on the table. 6. Open both grippers and withdraw. Check that the release has returned, the head remains locked and the cartridge rests inside the tray.
