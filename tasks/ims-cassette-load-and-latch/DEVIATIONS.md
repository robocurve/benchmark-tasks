# DEVIATIONS (from the draft task and its physics proof)

1. Start state: the explorer is misloaded across the cassette (handle along x on the front rim, at y 4.7 cm), the
   maker's "improperly placed instruments" case; the mirror lies on a tray in front. The draft had both loose
   instruments crossed over the seated ones; one misload is enough to block the lid and keeps the start repeatable.
   The explorer lies outside the band of the lid's pads, which swing back over the hinge when the lid is open.
2. Rail grip: one sprung silicone lip per rail at every notch (draft proof: lumped jaws, 1 N/mm), 2.5 mm into the
   10.5 mm mouth (one lip per rail, on the notch's +x side; a 9.5 mm handle must push it back about 1.3-1.5 mm) at 3 N/mm. A smaller overlap passed in the soft-contact pre-check by
   contact compliance alone and jammed in Isaac; at this overlap both engines have to push the lip back. The lid's weight
   on a resting handle does not push it home (control: explorer left across, 40 s, lid stays open).
3. All five instruments are loose bodies; the three pre-seated ones are held only by the rail lips, and the demo
   presses each of them home after taking the explorer off them.
4. The cassette is fixed in a low bench holder (draft: loose on the table), so the lid push and the clip press cannot
   slide it.
5. Lid opens to 120 deg (draft: flat behind): leaning back on its stop, a sweep from the front cannot knock it shut,
   and the open-loop demo can still push it over from behind.
6. Clip: over-centre lever with a hook tab (draft proof: friction pivot). A friction-held clip crept shut on its own;
   over centre, gravity holds it open on its stop and holds it locked after the push. The hook tab catches under a
   keeper lip that is its own fixed body (the clip is a link of the cassette articulation, and one articulation's
   links do not collide); a 2 N lid pull lifts the hooked lid 0.76 deg and the unhooked lid 10 deg.
7. Roles: the left arm takes out the misloaded explorer, turns it 90 deg in the air (wrist 0 -> -90 deg) and seats it
   in slot 4, and later closes the lid; the right arm seats the mirror in slot 2 and hooks the clip. One arm holding
   the lid while the other works the clip made the elbows collide.
8. Each handle is pressed at its middle (y = 0), between the rails; pressed near one rail, one end stayed on its lips.
   The press aims 30 mm below the seated handle top (closed finger tips, up to about 13 N).
9. The tray has short cradle ridges 5.5 mm either side of each handle place, near the handle ends: a round handle on
   a flat tray rolled off its front edge in Isaac.
10. With lips at every notch the inner teeth are cut down under their lip; each keeps a rounded cap at the same
    height (a square corner held a handle 5 mm above its seat in Isaac).
11. After their last task contact both arms keep their fingers closed while parking (no effect on the end state).
12. Handles 130 mm (shanks 17.5 mm, 165 mm overall): with 120 mm handles over rails 120 mm apart, a handle 4 mm off
