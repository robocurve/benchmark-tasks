# Fold a travel hair dryer's handle

Folding a compact hair dryer reduces its volume before drawer or suitcase storage. The bounded mechanical operation ends once the folded device rests released on the counter.

## Instruction

Fold the unplugged travel hair dryer's handle fully against its barrel. Leave the dryer resting on the counter with the handle folded, release both hands, and let it settle. The remote part of the cable is already coiled and tied; the loose tail remains attached to the handle. Do not pull or remove the cable.

Control and 14 joint/jaw targets are 20 Hz; physics is 240 Hz. Policy inputs are three stock 320 × 176 RGB images and 14 measured joint/jaw positions. The cool, unplugged dryer has a 154 × 140 × 79 mm unfolded envelope and 368 g body mass excluding cable/nozzle, based on Panasonic EH-NA2C specifications. Plastic-like barrel and grip bodies are 285 g and 83 g. A metallic-looking pin/eye/clevis surrounds the passive native Y hinge, with a head-local anchor (35,0,-50) mm, 0–90 degree stops and no angle target. The authored barrel origin is (280,-100,45) mm, rolled +90 degrees about X; the unfolded handle origin is (315,-50,45) mm. Free bodies settle during reset; the recorded seed-0 barrel starts near (276.818,-90.463,37.414) mm. Six rubber-like cable capsules, each 50 mm long, radius 2.5 mm and mass 1.25 g, form an attached 300 mm loose tail. Its exact reset pose and all six cable poses are generated by layout.RESET_POSES. No body is anchored. Geometry, material contact properties, mass allocation and internal hinge details are engineering estimates. The remote cable is already tied, a storage precondition. This bounded task ends with the folded dryer resting released on the counter; packing a bag is excluded.

## Scoring

Score the current hinge fold from its open position: below 45° earns 0, 45° or more earns 0.4, and the folded zone (80–93°, around the 90° storage stop) earns 0.7. Award 1.0 once the intact dryer rests entirely on the counter, both hands are clear of the dryer and cord, and the handle stays in that zone for one continuous second. During that second each visible barrel end must stay within 1 cm of where it began. Restart the clock on loss of fold, release or support, or from a new rest position if the barrel moves farther. Detachment, leaving the counter or invalid simulation input overrides all credit with 0.

## Action limit

1,000 actions at 20 Hz (50 simulated seconds), physics 240 Hz.

## Fidelity and estimates

Original procedural 154×140×79 mm, 368 g body based on Panasonic EH-NA2C specifications; hinge and component geometry, mass allocation, inertia, friction, bound coil and cable dimensions are engineering estimates. Passive native joints, real collision contacts and massive attached cord are simulated. Unplugged state is a scene precondition. No electrical, thermal or airflow simulation.

## Reset

Single deterministic fixed scene, seed 0. Only reset writes actor transforms and velocities; the initial free device and cable settle naturally. Runtime object transforms, attachment, material, mass and geometry are never changed.
