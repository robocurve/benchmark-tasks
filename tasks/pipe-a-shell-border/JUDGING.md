# Judge the corner border

Use the full native video, a top photograph and close side/detail photographs
with a millimetre ruler. Follow the final material; commanded targets do not count.
The guide is 90 mm, then 60 mm at right angles. Its corridor extends 9 mm each
side and 2 mm beyond the two outer ends.

Zero for a gouge over 1 mm, robot/icing contact, incomplete evidence, or icing at least 0.5 mm thick covering over 20 mm² outside the 18 mm-wide guide corridor (2 mm end allowance). Otherwise 0.25/0.5/0.75 for at least 35/70/110 mm of valid shells. Each head is 6–16 mm wide and 2.5–8 mm high, with a connected tail 4–22 mm after it, at least 1 mm wide and 0.5 mm high, no more than 3 mm high or 65% of head width/height. Head-to-tail length is at least 1.25 times head width; heads are at least 6 mm apart. The visible footprint midline must stay within 4 mm of the guide. Full credit requires at least 135 mm (80 mm on the first side, 50 mm on the second), nine valid shells, gaps at most 2 mm, a joined corner and two visible ridges per counted shell, separated by 1.5 mm and continuous for 2 mm. For the last second, the released dispenser is 5 mm from the robot and 20 mm from the border, moving at most 2 mm. Otherwise award the highest lower row. Use photos, video and a millimetre ruler.

Measure straightness from the midpoint between the visible left and right
edges of each shell footprint. It must remain within 4 mm of the corresponding
guide section. At the corner, check that a continuous visible icing footprint
joins the two sides; two separate straight strips do not receive full credit.
Count the corner length only once. Ridge crests and their separating groove
must be visible in ordinary close photographs; no groove-depth measurement is
required. Sum the lengths occupied by valid head-and-tail shapes without
counting overlaps twice. A plain line or disconnected round blobs cannot pass.

The retained per-section shape tests are supplemented by tests showing that the
old straight-only success cannot pass this revised task. Synthetic or replay
checks do not replace the new physical robot demonstration.
