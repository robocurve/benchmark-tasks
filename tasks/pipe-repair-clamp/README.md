# Stop a leaking pipe with a hinged repair clamp: set it over the split, squeeze its halves shut until it latches

On a plumbing training board on the bench, a 3/4 in copper pipe runs left to right between two pipe stands, with a red
band round it where it is split. Beside it stands an open hinged pipe repair clamp. The robot is told to clamp the
leak on the pipe with the repair clamp.

A split pipe is clamped until it can be replaced: the clamp's lining has to be centred on the split, the clamp set
over the pipe, and its two halves squeezed shut round the pipe until its latch snaps. The task tests finding where a
part belongs on a long fixture from a visual mark, setting a sprung, hinged clamp over it, and closing it until it
locks.

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `task.py` | The yam_arena task file: the pipe and its stands, the hinged clamp, `success()` and `score()` |
| `partkit.py` | Rigid parts built from boxes and cylinders, with hinges, for yam_arena task files |
| `meshkit.py` | Meshes built in code (here the pipe), with their colliders |
| `demo.py` | The scripted full-score demo and its negative variants |

## Run a trial

In the Isaac Lab-Arena environment from the simulation walkthrough, from this folder:

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy dream \
  -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 \
  --instruction "Clamp the leak on this pipe with the repair clamp." \
  --scorer task_score --max-steps 1200 --store-frames --log-dir dream_out
```

## Scripted demo

```bash
python demo.py task.py demo_out                           # full score: writes demo_*_cam.mp4, setup.png, result.json
DEMO_VARIANT=offleak python demo.py task.py offleak       # clamped 100 mm from the leak: 0.5
DEMO_VARIANT=nosqueeze python demo.py task.py nosqueeze   # set over the leak, left open: 0.25
```

The right arm pinches the open clamp by its hinge knuckle, carries it over the pipe, centres it on the red band and
lowers it until its halves straddle the pipe. It lets go, opens its fingers wide round the halves, lowers them to the
grip pads and closes them, squeezing both halves in under the pipe until the latch snaps, then moves away. The demo
reads only the layout and its own joints.

## Scoring

Evaluated every control step; the score at the end of the episode counts (`SCORE_OVER = "end"`).

- 1.0 (success, which must hold for 1 s): latched shut round the pipe (both halves within 2 degrees of shut, bore axis
  within 4 mm of the pipe's and lined up within 10 degrees) and centred over the leak (within 12 mm of the red
  band), both grippers 30 mm or more away, nothing moving.
- 0.75: latched over the leak, but a gripper is near or it still moves.
- 0.5: latched round the pipe, but not over the leak.
- 0.25: the clamp sat on the pipe over the leak at some point.
- Otherwise 0.

## Runtime and seeds

About 1 minute per trial for the full 1,200 steps, plus Isaac Sim start-up. The layout is fixed (`object_jitter=0`);
there are no seeds to choose.

## Fidelity

Rigid bodies at true size; the pipe is an exact mesh, the clamp's half shells are steel staves hinged on a pin. The halves' opening spring and the snap latch are drives on the hinges: free under the spring until both are squeezed to within 2 degrees of shut round the pipe, then held shut. The rubber lining's seal and the water are not simulated. All geometry is generated by code in the package.

## Real-world build

- A 400 mm length of 3/4 in copper pipe with a 20 mm band of red tape at the split mark, held in two pipe stands
  screwed to a board.
- A 3/4 in hinged pipe repair clamp (about 75 mm long, rubber-lined, snap latch).

Check the result by eye and by hand: the clamp latched, centred on the red band.

All geometry is generated by code in the package; no third-party assets are used.
