Mechanism the ability needs (real): each grip's spring-closed jaw must be held open by its lever (to 6.2 mm of the
real 0-6.4 mm) while a 3.2 mm tab goes in: grip A's sideways through the open side of the thru slot, grip B's by
jogging the crosshead (LEFT key held) along the axis over the other tab; letting the lever go clamps the tab. A jaw cannot close onto a tab that is not lying flat in the slot (it rests on it higher, or on nothing). The tab must lie flat on the 19.1 mm serrated lower jaw (within 4 mm of its centre line),
at least 17 mm deep past the jaw face, and the dogbone must not be pushed onto a backstop
(a visible 2 mm gap at each slot end, see DEVIATIONS.md). The task ends with the specimen mounted: no slack take-up or test load.

Recognisability at video size (320 x 176 top camera, ~4 mm/px): dogbone 165 mm ~ 30-33 px long (measured), ivory on a dark mat;
test-stand bed 460 mm ~ 110 px; grip housings 38 x 62 mm ~ 9 x 15 px each with red lever handles; the 3 mm jaw gap is not visible from above (proxy: SUBACTION_END + close-up, README). Nothing floats: the
dogbone rests on the tray rests; the jaw carriages rest on their slides.
