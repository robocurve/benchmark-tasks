# cedar-shoe-tree-insert-and-seat build deviations

1. **Rigid compound oxford upper + hollow multi-facet heel pocket**: Instead of deformable finite-element calfskin leather, the 380 g size-10 cap-toe oxford is modelled as a 39-primitive rigid body with a 16 mm stacked outsole/welt/insole deck, an 84 mm clear forepart chamber under a 62 mm cap-toe ceiling, 68 mm outer brogue shoulders behind a 50 mm tapered cap-toe clamping nose, low-cut 45.5 mm ankle quarters, and a 66 mm clear curved heel pocket topped by an 8 mm inward topline collar undercut (`z = 52..70 mm`, `270 mm` clear throat vs `278 mm` cavity). Because `SelfCollisions=False` within articulations, the shoe (`SHOE`) and twin-tube shoe tree (`TREE`) are separate parts so every insertion guide, toe stop, collar lip, and counter pocket lock acts through true inter-body rigid contact.
2. **Calibrated prismatic spring joint for twin brass tubes**: The twin parallel brass guide tubes and stainless inner rods are rendered visually as paired cylinders on the 145 g forepart and 175 g heel slide (`320 g` total, both links well above the `25 g` floor) and coupled mechanically by one coaxial `Y`-axis prismatic joint (`spring_tube`, `42 mm` travel, `250.0 N/m`, `5.5 N` at the `22 mm` compression needed to clear the `270 mm` collar lip, settling to `~18.5 mm` / `4.6 N` active preload in the seated state). Pushing with one arm alone while the other arm stays parked slides the unheld 380 g leather outsole (`mu = 0.12`, `~0.45 N` friction) across the mat with `< 4 mm` spring compression so the heel block never clears the collar (`score = 0.0` statelessly from end-state poses, needing zero gripper-on-shoe proximity checks in `score()`).
3. **Front bolster on upright cedar handle crest**: Added a `44 x 8 x 36 mm` front cedar push bolster `30 mm` ahead of the `28 x 36 mm` upright grip crest (`HANDLE_Y = -0.192 m`, bolster at `-0.162 m`, `7 mm` clear of the `38 mm` finger pad span during pick) plus `+22 mm` forward compression overdrive so the axial push clears `-131 mm` cleanly under differential-IK arm compliance across `0.8x` to `1.5x` friction and soft-contact perturbations.
4. **Stateless scorer + bimanual IK workspace**: `components(objects)` and `score(objects)` are 100% stateless (`SUCCESS_HOLD_S = 3.0`), reading only physical poses, `spring_tube` travel, and live gripper clearance.
