Lower the blue weight of the ratchet trainer onto its wooden rest in two stages, stopping at a middle tooth in between. 1. Grip the orange crank handle and turn it slightly upward to take the load off the pawl. 2. With the other gripper, press the orange pawl tab down and keep it down. 3. Turn the crank slowly to lower the lever, never faster than 40 degrees per second, until the lever is just above horizontal. 4. Let the pawl tab back up, then ease the crank down until the pawl catches the next tooth, with the lever 1 to 10 degrees above horizontal (the second tooth below the start). 5. Open both grippers and move them away: the lever must stay there on the pawl for at least 2 seconds. 6. Take the crank again, take in slightly, press the pawl tab down and lower the lever slowly until the weight rests on the wooden rest. 7. Let the pawl tab go, release the crank and move both grippers away.
