microwave.usdc
  Source: ds_R001_06, Microwave With Push-Button Release
  Changes: the task mounts the oven to the world and configures the release,
  latch, hinge damping and timer through the simulation.

steel_bowl.usdc
  Source: ds_R078_01, Large Steel Mixing Bowl
  Changes: scaled to a shallow bowl, with a rolled rim and a flat foot.

glass_bowl.usdc
  Source: ds_R078_02, Glass Mixing Bowl
  Changes: scaled to an 18.5 cm outer diameter and 8.1 cm height.
