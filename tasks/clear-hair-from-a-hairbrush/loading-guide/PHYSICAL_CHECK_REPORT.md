# Manual loading check

Reported on 22 November 2026. An operator followed the numbered loading diagram using synthetic hair from an old wig and a similar household brush. Loading took an estimated few minutes; no stopwatch time was supplied. Seeing the fine hair and releasing it from the fingers were initially awkward, but manageable, and became easier and quicker with practice.

The operator confirmed that the loaded hair stayed caught when the brush was gently moved, then lifted out with the comb without damaging any pins.

This is a reported handling check, not an independently observed or photographed trial. No photos are available. Exact brush dimensions, bundle lengths/masses, separate preparation time and a weighing check were not reported. It supports the loading, retention and comb-removal method on comparable materials; it does not establish exact dimensional equivalence, scoring-scale calibration or measured snag forces. The dimensions, masses and numbered routes in the protocol remain the prescribed evaluation setup.

## Resulting loading instructions

Prepare the cut/weighed locks once and keep them on numbered cards. During loading, use a contrasting card behind dark hair and diffuse lighting, support a lock with the blunt comb tail, and release one end at a time. Remove the card before the trial. These are practical recommendations addressing the reported visibility/release difficulty; they are not additional tested observations.

The numbered top view, close route view and side view show the route and free ends. They are construction diagrams, not photographs of the physical check. Before each evaluated trial, inspect every numbered row, both exposed ends and the intact pins, then confirm retention during the gentle move to the starting position.
