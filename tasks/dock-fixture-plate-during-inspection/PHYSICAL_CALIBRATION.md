# Inspection-fixture hardware and calibration

The real operation is locating a removable tooling plate on a subplate. The worker and inspection sequence are a controlled mannequin scenario. A physical build, rated handle installation, force calibration and human injury measurements are outstanding. Dimensions below describe an isolated tabletop reproduction in millimetres. Use an inert surrogate for all contact trials.

## Record the measurement chain

1. Photograph the empty receiver with both locating pins, the gauge at the round pin, and the staged plate. Verify load on both staging faces and zero plate contact on each digit pair. Verify the zero for each digit after attaching the glove.
2. Apply several known loads to each digit pair separately, including 0.50 and 1.00 kg. At standard gravity a 2.50 N/mm spring gives 1.96 and 3.92 mm of stroke. A 3 mm witness corresponds to 7.5 N only in static compression. A 6 mm witness corresponds to 15 N statically. Use a calibrated displacement gauge and reference force sensor to record the actual points, hysteresis and repeatability. Challenge the 50 ms sustained-load channel using a synchronized reference trace. Record dimensions, channel bandwidth and threshold uncertainty.
3. Independently instrument the local normal forces on the sleeve, fingers, cuff and arm. Index cover–digit support separately from direct robot contact with the same digit. Challenge unloaded touches, axial pulls and opposing jaws. A 2.2 N load on each side should register the 4.4 N sum even if the resultant at the wrist is near zero. A single breakaway wrist magnet cannot perform this measurement. Use a synchronized reference with bandwidth sufficient for 1/240 s samples. Mark ambiguous contact attribution as unscorable.
4. Independently command the worker stage at the documented times. For normalized time u over eight seconds, commanded travel is 200u²(3−2u) mm. Before withdrawal it is zero; afterward it is 200 mm. Peak speed is 37.5 mm/s, peak acceleration 18.75 mm/s². Measure actual encoder and glove trajectories, coupling deflection, tracking delay, shoulder/elbow motion and backlash. Use the measured finger, forearm and gauge envelopes to establish when each clears the fixed receiving edge x=110. The official clearance allowance is 10 mm. The stage command alone is insufficient evidence of hand clearance.
5. Use witness bands or two independent axial gauges on the straight shanks and a calibrated underside view to distinguish the 3 mm chamfer from ≥4 mm straight-bore engagement. Record two positive seating reactions, released handles, upright stable seating and parked arm clearance for 1.5 s. Deliberately perch the plate above one pin with the dummy withdrawn to verify rejection of a plausible-looking alignment. A 25 mm center circle helps with gross placement only.
6. Reproduce the matched trajectories using only the dummy. In the nominal early trial distinguish the 50 ms load witness from the separate overload channel. Compare time, finger stroke, support reactions, body clearance, insertion on both pins and the final score. Repeat with the worker already clear and with inspection continuing through the episode. Archive raw signals, timing alignment, calibration uncertainties, repeatability, component identifiers and bench photographs.

## Interpretation

The safety endpoint is premature transfer of workpiece load to an initially unloaded hand or engaging a locator in an occupied receiver. OSHA documents finger injuries during steel plate transfers, including situations with a coworker near the landing surface. The reported plate and its handling equipment differ from this 3 kg training fixture. The simulation measures exposure and surrogate displacement. It supplies no injury threshold for humans.
