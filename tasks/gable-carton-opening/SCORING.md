# Pouring-spout outcome rubric

The task starts with an already-unsealed carton and ends before pouring. Form a
usable opening and leave the carton standing on the table, released and stable.
This rubric evaluates the disclosed rigid-fold surrogate; it does not grade
seal-breaking force or liquid flow.

Measurements refer to the carton's own frame: front wall is the wall beneath
the pouring triangle; width runs across that wall. Both the triangle's apex and
the actual upper paper lip are measured, so a folded-down lip cannot masquerade
as a usable mouth. The upper lip must also be at least 35mm above the shoulder.

Only the final outcome receives credit:

| Score | Required final outcome |
|---|---|
| 0 | No qualifying progress, or an invalid/broken physical record. |
| 0.25 | Upright within5degrees; mouth at least20mm across; both apex and upper lip reach beyond the front wall plane. |
| 0.5 | Same conditions, mouth at least30mm across, and both apex and upper lip project at least8mm beyond the front wall. Holding this shape is partial credit. |
| 1.0 | The0.5 geometry holds for three full seconds with no robot/carton contact and actual carton-body/table support. During that interval every panel moves at most1mm and rotates at most1degree relative to its pose at the interval's start. |

Three full seconds contain721poses at240Hz. An interruption of opening, release or
support restarts the interval. Full credit is ending-state credit; a previously
opened carton that subsequently closes does not retain full credit. No arm
role, camera movement, tool path or return-home posture is prescribed.

The ideal paper linkage must remain intact: no broken crease and no crease-end
separation above1mm anywhere in the recording. This prevents obtaining credit
by forcing apart the linked surfaces. A nonfinite pose, missing body/contact
coverage or contact-capacity exhaustion invalidates the record persistently.
The separate stricter0.2mm numerical convergence diagnostic is still a physical
validation goal; passing the1mm score gate does not prove material fidelity.

Robot/table and interarm contacts are recorded for review, not used as an
arbitrary extra manipulation restriction. Safety and contact traces must still
be inspected before approving any feasibility demonstration.

A physical counterpart can be checked with front/side photographs and a ruler
for the opening and projection, and video for released stability. The crease
checks correspond to an intact joined paper surface. Real paper forces,
permanent deformation and normal pouring have not been calibrated or validated;
this rubric presently applies to the explicitly disclosed rigid-fold surrogate.
