# Low-cost passive presenter reconstruction

The trial begins after printing; no airport terminal purchase is necessary.
Use a preprinted 430 × 50 mm cold-seal strip and an ordinary small suitcase.
A passive outlet holds the trailing end and allows a one-hand pull to extract it.
The screen and kiosk shell in the simulation are visual context only.

The dimensioned construction is in `task.py` (`self_bag_drop_kiosk`). A practical
sheet-material guide can use two 350 × 82 mm rigid smooth plates, with 21 mm
spacers between them, a rear stop and a 12 mm lower outlet lip. Mount the outlet
240 mm above the counter. Feed 340 mm of the strip into the channel and leave
90 mm beyond the mouth; no projecting rods support that free end. Fit a soft
spring paper-retention clip at the trailing end, wholly inside the channel.
Use a small pull scale to set retention to approximately 2 N; the paper should
pull free before tearing. This is a proposed physical reconstruction, not a
measured commercial printer or a validated manufactured fixture. Keep the clip
and any fasteners away from the robot's accessible outlet.

The main scored operation uses ordinary cold-seal luggage stock and the
suitcase's existing closed side handle; the presenter does not guide the tag
under the handle or seal it. The stock must match the self-bonding, no-liner
process. Do not substitute ordinary sticky-backed labels without adding their
real liner-removal step to the task.
