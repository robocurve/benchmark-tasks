# Physical trial protocol

## Two-level bay and stock tabletop mounting

The simulator uses a stock tabletop configuration on the upper level of a two-level bay. The lower floor is the zero datum. Mounting coordinates and simulated clearance are listed below. All dimensions are in metres.

| Datum | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Upper deck surface | Z = 1.1325 |
| Deck front edge | X = 0.080 |
| Tabletop width × depth | 1.200 × 0.800 |
| Tabletop front edge | X = 0.100 |
| Stock YAM mounting planes | X = 0, Y = ±0.305, Z = 1.880 |
| Rod axis | X = 0.414, Z = 1.978 |
| Nominal hook seating line | X = 0.378, Z = 1.929 |

Use an existing qualified bay and bench, with the tabletop surface 0.7475 m above the upper deck. Bolt the two stock YAM base plates to the work surface using the supplied hardware and a bench attachment approved for the combined operating and stopping reactions. Retain the 98 mm rod-to-mount height difference and 610 mm base spacing. Keep both the deck edge and the bench behind the sheet, and survey the unobstructed drop and swing envelope across the entire 1,828.8 mm liner. Keep guarding and cables outside the workspace. The bench dimensions describe the simulation, and the physical hardware selection depends on the available bay. [I2RT mounting guidance](https://doc.i2rt.com/products/yam)

The physical installation record must identify the existing deck, qualified bench and mounting drawings, base plates and attachment hardware, supporting substrate and approved anchors, and any costs added for the installation. Record the combined dynamic loads the installation is approved to carry, actual vertical datums, mounting torque and fastener engagement from the approved drawings, and the inspected sweep and drop clearance. Confirm protected access to the lower bay and required upper-deck guarding in the facility review. The simulation verifies the arrangement’s task geometry. A qualifying physical site, its hardware approval and its installed cost remain unverified.

## End-of-trial inspection

Photograph the dry liner, grommet hems, rod seats and flanges before reset. Mark each original flange-to-wall interface and the rod insertion at each flange. Mark a fixed reference on the wall visible in the recording. After the robot clears, compare the same views. Invalidate trials with a torn liner, separated reinforced hem, pulled grommet, permanent elongation visible against the references, bent hook, shifted flange, slipped rod, or a loose fastener. Inspect either side of the liner so a fold cannot hide damage. Record which witness changed.

## Transient strain

Before installation, mark adjacent measurement points across the film at each of the nine sampled heights, aligned with the twelve attachment columns. Measure the unloaded center-to-center separation of neighboring column marks at each height with the liner laid flat. During the trial reconstruct those distances with calibrated synchronized views. For a pair with baseline distance d, invalidate any measured separation above 1.17 × d. Keep the greatest extension, pair ID and frame. Use the pre/post inspection to record damage independently. A trial with occluded markers or an uncertainty interval crossing the limit requires another recording.

## Transient fixture force

Instrument both rod support flanges with calibrated three-axis reaction transducers. Preserve flange geometry and anchoring. Record simultaneously at 200 Hz or faster. Tare with the empty installed rod and hooks. Transform both reactions to the same frame, sum the vectors and take the magnitude. Invalidate when the magnitude exceeds 40 N continuously for more than 0.15 s. Record peak load, duration and the calibration check. The threshold is a scoring limit, independent of any fixture rating. The witness marks and post-trial inspection also catch permanent displacement after load has returned to baseline. A recording without force measurements can establish visible integrity, while transient overload remains unverified.

## Attachment and release

Check continuously that the ten original grommets stay threaded. Verify each repaired eye lies below its assigned hook cap and is visibly loaded after both hands withdraw. Record at least one second of hands-clear retention with all twelve attachments supported. Score incomplete trials according to the correctly supported repaired eyes and apply the physical invalidations above.
