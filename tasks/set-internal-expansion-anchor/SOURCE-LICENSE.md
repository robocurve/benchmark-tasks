# Sources and license

The original geometric solid definitions, engineering equivalent, scripted demo and scorer are distributed under the included MIT license. The generic task adapter retains its MIT notice in `native/LICENSE`. No downloaded CAD, manufacturer drawing, manual, product photograph or material-data PDF is redistributed.

The anchor and setting tool modelled are stock catalogue parts: the Simpson Strong-Tie DIAB37 drop-in anchor and its DIABST37 hand setting tool. The hole size, anchor length, bore and tool weight follow the published catalogue data. The sleeve slits, cone plug, tool pin, hammer and support dimensions are original definitions written for this task, because the maker does not publish them. No maker drawing or CAD is copied, and no load rating of the product is reproduced or implied. Product names are used only to identify the hardware a real trial needs.
