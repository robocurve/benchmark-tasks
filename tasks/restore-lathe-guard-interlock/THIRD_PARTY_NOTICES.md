# Asset provenance

The chuck, guard, operator surrogate, bench insert and remaining fixture geometry are authored in fixture.py and build_asset.py. The two USD files are generated from those sources. The station observation bridge in scenekit.py is adapted from the RoboCurve lock-before-loading task. The robot uses the installed YAM station. The independent reproduction directory carries the YAM diagnostic meshes and license. See YAM_LICENSE.txt.
