# flip-mount-hinge-seat-and-detent build deviations

1. **Sprung brass post staging detent + horizontal cam-clamp lever**: Instead of finite-element split-collar metal flexure, the 64 g anodized collar slides vertically along the 12.7 mm post (`2.6 N` drag) from an 11.2 mm staging position supported by a separate 28 g sprung brass detent (`520 N/m`, 22 deg taper -> zero idle gravity creep and `< 0.6 mm` spawn settle) and locks with a 32 g horizontal brass cam-clamp lever rotating about a vertical hinge axis (`0.32 N m` drag, zero gravity torque).
2. **Separate 32 g lower brass detent plunger with 16 deg low-drag cam ramp**: Modelled the 90-degree flip mount's lower detent as a cousin rigid body (`110 N/m` return spring, `0.0` joint friction) carrying a 16 deg cam ramp and a 3.5 mm horizontal brass catch lip opposing the 44 g filter holder's `0.28 N m/rad` upward return spring. Releasing before the striker passes the ramp springs the holder back to ~89 deg (`score = 0.50`); latching locks it at `~0.9 deg` across `0.8x–1.5x` friction and soft-contact checks.
3. **36 x 42 x 20 mm side actuator block & rear stabilizer boss**: Sized the filter holder's side knurled actuator block (`>= 20 mm` height) and the pedestal's rear 28 x 32 x 20 mm stabilizer boss at `(0.300, 0.135) m` (`160 mm` Y spacing from the post) so the left gripper clamps cleanly along world X (`yaw = -90 deg`, `0.08 mm` IK error) while 1-hand actuation shifts the unbraced 310 g post assembly `27 mm > 16 mm` out of its datum pocket (`score = 0.0`). `hands_clear()` explicitly guards the post, rear stabilizer boss, collar, clamp lever, filter ring, and side actuator block (`> 120 mm`).
