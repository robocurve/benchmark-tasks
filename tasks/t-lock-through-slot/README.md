# Pull a handle out through a slotted panel

A task file for the `yam_arena` embodiment ([yam-isaac-lab-arena](https://github.com/kairosiann/yam-isaac-lab-arena), Isaac Lab-Arena 0.3.1).

An orange T-handle on the robot's side of a steel panel runs on a rod through a narrow vertical slot. Pulling it stops dead after 2.5 cm: behind the panel, a T-shaped head lies across the slot and cannot pass. The handle cannot turn the head, because the head turns freely on the rod. The other arm must reach over the panel and turn the head, against its spring, while the first arm pulls. Two arms are required: the head's spring undoes the turn, and the rod's spring undoes the pull, if either arm lets go too early.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `task.py` | The task file: objects, the catch, the failures that stay failed, `score()`, `success()`, `demo()` |
| `scene_assets.py` | The panel (fixed) and the rod, handle, head and pin (an articulation), written as plain-text USD when the task loads |
| `scenekit.py`, `usdkit.py` | Helpers: USD bodies and articulations on `yam_arena`; meshes, materials and colliders as USD text |

## Run a trial

From this folder, in a shell with the `yam_arena` environment active.

Dream-1:

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy dream \
  -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 \
  --instruction "Pull the orange handle out to its stop." \
  --scorer task_score --max-steps 1200 --store-frames --log-dir runs/dream
```

Scripted demo (scores 1.0): the same command with `--policy yam_scripted`. Scripted failure (scores 0, the left arm pulls alone and is blocked): add `TASK_DEMO=failure` in front of the command.

The run prints `task_score` at the end: 1.0 once `success()` has held for 1 s, otherwise `score()` at the end of the run.

## How the simulation judges each rule

- **Travel:** the rod's sliding joint. The head enters the slot at 3.5 cm, and the catch engages at 9.5 cm.
- **Upright:** the head's turning joint within 10 degrees of its upright position. Its stop is 1 degree past upright.
- **Fingertips:** the middle of each finger's tip, against boxes around the rod with its handle, and the head with its pin.

## Runtime and seeds

- Demo: 298 steps (14.9 s simulated), about 3 minutes including start-up.
- Dream-1: 1,200 steps (60 s simulated), about 5 minutes.
- `object_jitter=0` keeps the layout fixed, so no seed is needed. The scripted demo is open loop, with timed waits, and deterministic.

## Sources

All meshes are generated by `scene_assets.py`. Sizes come from common products: a 1 mm painted steel service panel and a quarter-turn T-handle latch.
