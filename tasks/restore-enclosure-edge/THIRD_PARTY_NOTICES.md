# Asset provenance

The fixture, surrogate, USD generator, controller and scorer were authored for this task. Product names identify engineering references. The geometry is an independent approximation. The observation bridge in scenekit.py derives from the RoboCurve benchmark repository's lock-before-loading task. The station and robot are provided by the installed YAM environment. Independent diagnostic meshes and their original license accompany the reproduction package in diagnostics/yam_meshes.
