# Vaccinate a training arm and cover the site straight away

A task file for the `yam_arena` embodiment ([yam-isaac-lab-arena](https://github.com/kairosiann/yam-isaac-lab-arena), Isaac Lab-Arena 0.3.1).

An injection-training arm lies on a cushion, with a target circle on its upper arm, an injector pen in a holder, a gauze pad on a tray and a sharps bin. The robot is told to give the injection. The instruction does not name the aftercare: the pad must land within 4 s of the pen coming out, and the used pen must go in the bin. One arm alone cannot do it in time, because it must put the pen down first. A second arm that holds the pad and does nothing scores 0.5.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `task.py` | The task file: objects, the timers, the failures that stay failed, `score()`, `success()`, `demo()` |
| `scene_assets.py` | The arm, skin pad, pen, holder, gauze, tray and bin, written as plain-text USD when the task loads |
| `scenekit.py`, `usdkit.py` | Helpers: USD bodies and articulations on `yam_arena`; meshes, materials and colliders as USD text |

## Run a trial

From this folder, in a shell with the `yam_arena` environment active.

Dream-1:

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy dream \
  -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 \
  --instruction "Give the injection at the marked spot on the training arm." \
  --scorer task_score --max-steps 1200 --store-frames --log-dir runs/dream
```

Scripted demo (scores 1.0): the same command with `--policy yam_scripted`. Scripted failure (scores 0.5, the second arm holds the pad and does nothing): add `TASK_DEMO=failure` in front of the command.

The run prints `task_score` at the end: 1.0 once `success()` has held for 1 s, otherwise `score()` at the end of the run.

## How the simulation judges each rule

- **Pressing:** the pen's tip within 1.5 cm of the circle's centre, and the skin pad's joint at least 1 cm in. A break resets the 5 s timer.
- **Touching the arm:** the pen's tip within 2 mm of the top of the upper arm or the forearm. Outside the circle, or after the first lift, this fails the trial.
- **Lifted:** the tip more than 5 mm above the skin pad's rest height, after a press.
- **Covered:** the gauze's centre within 1.5 cm of the circle's centre, its underside within 4 mm of the skin. **Pressed:** covered, and the skin pad in by at least 1 mm.
- **Fingertips:** the middle of each finger's tip, against boxes around the upper arm and the forearm.

## Runtime and seeds

- Demo: 476 steps (23.8 s simulated), about 4 minutes including start-up.
- Dream-1: 1,200 steps (60 s simulated), about 6 minutes.
- `object_jitter=0` keeps the layout fixed, so no seed is needed. The scripted demo is open loop with timed waits, and deterministic.

## Sources

All meshes are generated by `scene_assets.py`. Sizes come from common products: an injection-training arm, a 15 cm auto-injector pen, a 5 x 5 cm gauze pad, a small sharps bin.
