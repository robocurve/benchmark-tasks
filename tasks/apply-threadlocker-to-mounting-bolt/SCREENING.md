# Threadlocker application screening

Operation: prepare the engagement threads of a steel mounting bolt with medium-strength threadlocker before reassembly. The useful result is material distributed through the mating thread region, not an already cured or torque-qualified joint.

[Henkel's application instructions](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/products/industrial-adhesives/central-pdp.html/loctite-248/BP000000153639/variation/462476.html) support circumferential application and material in the thread roots. The [technical data sheet](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-248-en_GL.pdf) supports use of a semi-solid stick. [Henkel's threadlocking overview](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/uk/en/applications/threadlockers.html) documents industrial maintenance applications, including robots and gearboxes. These sources establish an actual operation; they do not establish an occupation-count threshold for this specific benchmark.

The same application is documented for automotive professionals in [Henkel's automotive maintenance guide, page 5](https://dm.henkel-dam.com/is/content/henkel/auto-maintenance-do-it-right.pdf): its through-hole fastener procedure explicitly includes a semi-solid stick applied to the engagement-thread roots. The benchmark therefore addresses fastener preparation shared across vehicle and machinery maintenance, rather than a model-specific robot service part. Assembly and curing are outside the stated application-only scope.

The task preserves standard bolt dimensions, helical contact geometry in the application zone, finite source material, native contact, robot force limits and visible deposition. It simplifies wax flow with a local contact-transfer law, and excludes chemistry and curing. The rectangular carrier is an explicitly dimensioned tool adaptation. The bolt may remain held by its clean head at completion; arbitrary post-application placement is not tested.
