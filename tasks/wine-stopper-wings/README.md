# Reseal opened sparkling wine with a two-clamp stopper: cap on the mouth, both side clamps squeezed shut under the lip

An opened 375 ml half bottle of sparkling wine stands on a bar mat, its cork gone. Beside it a chrome two-clamp
sparkling-wine stopper sits on a stopper peg, both side clamps open. The robot is told to reseal the bottle with the
stopper.

Bars that sell sparkling wine by the glass reseal the bottle between pours. The stopper's cap has to sit square on
the mouth, centred by its skirt round the lip, and both side clamps have to be squeezed in until their hooks click
under the ledge below the lip; a clamp left open lets the gas lift the stopper off. The task tests seating a cap on a
tall, loose bottle and then closing two hinged clamps on it without knocking the bottle or the cap off.

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `task.py` | The yam_arena task file: the bottle, the stopper and its side clamps, the peg, `success()` and `score()` |
| `partkit.py` | Rigid parts built from boxes and cylinders, with hinges, for yam_arena task files |
| `meshkit.py` | Meshes built in code (here the lathed bottle), with their colliders |
| `demo.py` | The scripted full-score demo and its negative variants |

## Run a trial

In the Isaac Lab-Arena environment from the simulation walkthrough, from this folder:

```bash
inspect-robots run --embodiment yam_arena --policy dream \
  -E task=task.py -E object_jitter=0 -E control_hz=20 -E cam_width=320 -E cam_height=176 \
  --instruction "Reseal the opened sparkling wine with the stopper." \
  --scorer task_score --max-steps 1200 --store-frames --log-dir dream_out
```

## Scripted demo

```bash
python demo.py task.py demo_out                         # full score: writes demo_*_cam.mp4, setup.png, result.json
DEMO_VARIANT=half python demo.py task.py half          # cap seated, clamps squeezed only partway: 0.25
DEMO_VARIANT=nopress python demo.py task.py nopress     # cap seated, both clamps left open: 0.25
```

The right arm, its hand tipped 30 degrees forward (the mouth is 236 mm up), takes the stopper by its top knob off the peg,
sets the cap on the mouth and lets go. It opens its fingers wide round the two side clamps, lowers them to the clamps'
grip pads and closes them, so the squeeze swings both clamps in until their hooks click under the lip. The demo reads only the layout and its own joints.

## Scoring

Evaluated every control step; the score at the end of the episode counts (`SCORE_OVER = "end"`).

- 0 if the bottle tips over 20 degrees or falls. This is permanent.
- 1.0 (success, which must hold for 1 s): the cap seated on the mouth (within 2.5 mm of its middle, 2 mm of its top
  and 5 degrees square) with both side clamps clicked shut, both grippers 30 mm or more away, nothing moving.
- 0.75: seated with both clamps shut, but a gripper is near or something still moves.
- 0.5: seated with one clamp shut, both grippers 30 mm or more away.
- 0.25: the cap was seated on the mouth at some point.
- Otherwise 0.

## Runtime and seeds

About 1 minute per trial for the full 1,200 steps, plus Isaac Sim start-up. The layout is fixed (`object_jitter=0`);
there are no seeds to choose.

## Fidelity

Rigid bodies at true size; the bottle is a lathed mesh with an exact (signed-distance) collider; the stopper's cap is a disc with a 12-stave skirt, its side clamps hinged plates. Each clamp's open spring and its catch are drives on its hinge: free under a light spring until squeezed to within 2 degrees of shut, then held shut. The gasket's seal and the gas pressure are not simulated; the hooks under the lip hold the cap down. All geometry is generated by code in the package.

## Real-world build

- A 375 ml sparkling-wine half bottle with a standard 29 mm crown lip, emptied to about a third and recorked for
  storage, or filled with water.
- A chrome two-clamp sparkling-wine stopper with a 42 mm cap and side clamps.
- A turned wooden stopper peg, 25 mm across and 80 mm tall, glued to a base, and a 150 mm bar mat.

Check the result by lifting the stopper gently by its knob: shut clamps hold it on the bottle.
