# Asset sources

The whiteboard, triangle strokes, wooden ruler, calculator meshes and textures
are generated by `model.py`. No downloaded commercial meshes, product
photographs, logos or textures are included.

The ruler has a 150 mm metric scale on a 160 × 26 × 12 mm wooden body. Its zero
mark is 5 mm from the end. The board is 230 × 200 × 6 mm, with a metal frame.
The calculator body is 101.6 × 139.7 mm, with 1.5 mm spring-return key travel.
Masses, friction and button spring constants are approximations, not product
measurements. The whiteboard is fixed; the ruler and calculator are free bodies.

Text labels are rasterized with DejaVu Sans Bold. The font binary is not bundled.
DejaVu modifications are public domain; the underlying Bitstream Vera font
license is included in `licenses/DejaVu.txt`.

The YAM workstation and calibrated cameras are supplied by the separately
installed `yam-isaac-lab-arena` dependency; its assets are not copied here.
