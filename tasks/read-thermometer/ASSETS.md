# Assets and licenses

The stand, passive screw and spring clamp, beaker, thermometer, timer, and their
procedural geometry and materials were created for this task and are covered by
the task's MIT license. The glassware and thermometer textures are generated
from dimensions and font glyphs. Reference product photographs are not distributed
or used as textures.

The calculator model and its key-label textures come from the MIT-licensed
Tape measure task. Its original notice is retained in
`assets/electronics/CALCULATOR-LICENSE.txt`.

DejaVu Sans supplies the printed scale, labels and timer lettering. Its license
is retained in `assets/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt`. Asset regeneration requires the system
font `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSans.ttf`; the generated assets run
without font installation.

The YAM robot, workstation and calibrated cameras are supplied by yam-arena.
They are referenced through that dependency and are not copied into this package.
OmniGlass is supplied by Isaac Sim.
