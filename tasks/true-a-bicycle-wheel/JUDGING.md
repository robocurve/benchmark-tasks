# Judging

After both hands and the spoke key are clear, observe at least one complete
revolution. Measure the full lateral excursion at a fixed rim pointer with
feeler gauges, radial excursion at a second pointer, and centring relative to
the axle with a ruler. Check all 32 spokes with a mechanical tensiometer and its
2 mm round-steel conversion chart. Record the measurements in millimetres and
newtons using the `wheel-judging-v2` fields in `scoring.py`.

Award 1.0 at lateral runout ≤0.5 mm, or 0.5 at ≤1.5 mm. Both require radial
runout ≤1 mm, dish error ≤0.5 mm and every spoke between 400 and 1,000 N.
Damage, direct rim bending, external rim support during the judged revolution,
incomplete evidence or any other outcome earns zero. Initial low spoke tension
prevents the unchanged setup earning credit.

For simulation, the judging record uses actual recorded rim geometry and the
elastic model's spoke tensions. These are explicitly simulated measurements.
Video review checks the released revolution, tool use and absence of damage.
