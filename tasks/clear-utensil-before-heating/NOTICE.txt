Wooden spatula geometry and texture maps adapted from NVIDIA SimReady Wooden_Spatula_B01 (Isaac 6.0 public asset catalogue), licensed CC BY 4.0. The original licence is included as LICENSE.txt.

Source asset:
https://omniverse-content-production.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/Assets/Isaac/6.0/Isaac/SimReady/Residential/Kitchen/Utensils/Wooden_Spatula_B01/sm_utensil_spatula_wooden_b01_01.usd

Changes: original x/y coordinates and physical scale retained; z shifted by +0.010070998221635818 m so the lowest mesh point is zero. Original polygon topology, normals and face-varying UVs retained. Texture maps reduced from 2048 to 1024 pixels; reduced normal map renormalized. Portable UsdPreviewSurface material uses original albedo/roughness/normal maps with explicit non-colour handling for roughness and normals, dielectric metallic=0. Removed source assembly layers, custom exporter/guide/grasp metadata, SDF collision and unreliable original mass/inertia. The initial CPU decomposition of the unchanged watertight source mesh produced 20 convex pieces. These were subsequently split by exact planar clipping into 163 convex pieces with at most 64 vertices per piece; the union and visual mesh were preserved. Mass/inertia recomputed for uniform wood density 700 kg/m3. The imported asset material retains source friction 0.5/0.5 and restitution 0.4; the task applies its disclosed engineering override of static/dynamic friction 0.8/0.6 at runtime. No shape scaling, handle, attachment or extra grip feature added.
