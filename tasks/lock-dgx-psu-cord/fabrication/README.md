# Rear panel fabrication

Export the OpenSCAD panel solid for laser cutting, routing or drawing extraction. Use 2 mm aluminum stock at 237 × 58 mm. The three inlet axes have 79 mm nominal pitch. Keep that spacing if fitting the panel to a separate stand. Install three Schurter 4798.9000 screw-mount C20 inlets with the single upper pin oriented as shown in the manufacturer drawing. Use 42 mm mounting centers and the manufacturer's 0.30 N·m maximum for the inlet mounting screws. Fasten the panel vertically at both ends to a rigid bench stand, with the inlet centers about 95 mm above the work surface. Use two inserted neighboring cords. Leave space behind the panel for the terminal enclosure and in front for the hand and the cable bend. Keep the entire bench disconnected. [Schurter drawing, 20 August 2026](https://www.schurter.com/en/datasheet/typ_4798.pdf)

Use the SSEMCO 620890 C19 system end as the target. SSEMCO specifies fit with standard C20 inlets. The commercial rotary switch geometry, internal operation and this specific inlet combination have not been physically tested here. The simulated pad is an original actuation proxy. [SSEMCO 620890 Rev. A](https://ssemco.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/620890-Series_M1-20191231.pdf)

The simulation includes an original staging slide and rotary pad to fit the Arena grasp envelope. The pad is on a rearward extension, with its center approximately 50 mm laterally, 25 mm above, and 62 mm behind the target plug datum when unlocked. The ring mass is balanced about its shaft for the simulation. These are original training design inputs. The physical trial uses the cord's own switch.
