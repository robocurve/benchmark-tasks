# apple-wedger-press-through-to-board build deviations

1. **8-wedge pre-scored geometry + prismatic core anvil**: Instead of deformable finite-element apple cutting, the apple is modelled as 8 distinct pie-wedge skin/flesh segments surrounding a 90 g central prismatic `cut` joint with 2.6–3.7 mm radial blade clearance and 4.7 mm central corer clearance when level, plus an irreversible 18 N (`4000 N/m`, 4.5 mm yield) continuous severing law. Pressing only one handle at +-69 mm tilts the wedger > 5 deg (measured 93.9 deg peak tilt / 51.0 deg end tilt, 36.7 mm partial cut jam, `score = 0.0` in single-hand offline physics check) and physically binds the 40 mm coring tube and 8 radial blades against the wedges without needing gripper-proximity checks in `score()`.
2. **Wing flange handles**: Added 32 x 54 x 7 mm horizontal wing flanges directly underneath the 20 mm-thick raised grip spines (`z = 0.047 m`) so downward cutting force transfers via normal bearing on the flange shelves rather than side-wall friction alone, holding clean margin across `0.8x / 1.25x / 1.5x` friction and soft-contact stress checks.
3. **Cradle pocket clearance & core bottom clearance**: Expanded the recessed board pocket from 74 mm to 77 mm around the 72 mm apple OD (`2.5 mm` radial margin) and sized the sliding core disc height to `4.5 mm` (`core_top = 0.0535 m`) so the core clears the well floor by `1.0 mm` at full `-48 mm` cut and the stainless outer ring rests flush on the cutting board deck.
4. **Open-loop wrist pitch compensation**: Added `AIM_DX = 0.0060 m` and `PRESS_OVERDRIVE = 0.026 m` in `demo()` to compensate YAM wrist (`kp = 10 N*m/rad`) and shoulder (`kp = 80 N*m/rad`) compliance under the 210 g wedger and 18 N cut force.
