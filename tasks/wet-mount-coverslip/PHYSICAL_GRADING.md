# Physical grading


Take an overhead close-up photo with a scale beside the slide. Overlay the central
12×12mm square about the specimen, divide it into0.5mm cells and classify visible
wet film versus dry/air areas. At least98% of that square must be wet for full
credit. Inspect the entire area covered by the glass, including outside the central square. Compare each enclosed air region with a1mm-diameter reference circle;
use area rather than requiring a circular bubble. Check that every cover edge
is on the slide and use the last second of video to verify release and stillness.
A clean flat placement can pass; there is no mandatory tilt or two-arm strategy.
The simulation's20µm gap is a constitutive regularization, not a physical grading
measurement. Use standard22mm coverslips, a6mm nonliving printed colour target,
and a10µL predispensed drop of lightly dyed water; avoid putting liquid on pads.

Use a disposable bench liner around the fixtures and keep the gripping surfaces
dry. Inspect consumables before each trial, use the same batch and room-temperature
preload, and clean/replenish them between runs. The trial uses no heating or live
biological sample. Handle slide edges with the normal glassware precautions.

`fabrication/grading-overlay.svg` provides the scoring grid and air-area
reference circle. Use it beside or over a scaled grading photo, never between
the slide and cover or as an edited policy-camera input.
