# Outcome rubric

After1s released, supported and still, with the cover entirely on the slide:1.0 if at least98% of the central12×12mm region is wet and no trapped-air component exceeds the area of a1mm-diameter circle;0.5 if at least50% is wet;otherwise0. Interarm collision gives0. Final state, not peak score.

The ending state is graded; a previous success does not survive a subsequent disturbance. No camera movement, arm allocation, named grasp, approach angle or scripted waypoint is scored. Native support, release and low motion must hold continuously for one second. Translational speed limit2mm/s and angular speed0.1rad/s are the numerical stillness convention. Nonfinite state and invalid contacts fail closed.

Material-response constants and limitations are in MATERIAL_MODEL.md. A final-frame photo and the last second of video document release/support and visible finish. Physical grading is specified in PHYSICAL_GRADING.md. Complete native demonstrations and Dream evaluation have been verified against the same scorer; their scores and run limits are recorded in task.json and README.md.

## Physical grading

Take an overhead close-up photo with a scale beside the slide. Overlay the central
12×12mm square about the specimen, divide it into0.5mm cells and classify visible
wet film versus dry/air areas. At least98% of that square must be wet for full
credit. Inspect the entire area covered by the glass, including outside the central square. Compare each enclosed air region with a1mm-diameter reference circle;
use area rather than requiring a circular bubble. Check that every cover edge
is on the slide and use the last second of video to verify release and stillness.
A clean flat placement can pass; there is no mandatory tilt or two-arm strategy.
The simulation's20µm gap is a constitutive regularization, not a physical grading
measurement. Use standard22mm coverslips, a6mm nonliving printed colour target,
and a10µL predispensed drop of lightly dyed water; avoid putting liquid on pads.
